About Vinehook

Vinehook is a Pixelsprout original. A paper lantern hangs from a night vine and swings over a river of hooks. Let go at the right moment and the lantern catches the next hook. A catch near the center of the hook starts a perfect chain. Miss, and the lantern falls into the dark.

How to play

  1. Tap the game, or press Space or Enter, to start and to let the lantern go.
  2. The vine swings on its own. Release when the lantern is lined up with the next hook.
  3. Catching near the center of a hook is a perfect and raises your chain. Off-center catches still score, but reset the chain to one.
  4. The swing gets quicker as the score climbs. Fall past the screen, or fly off the side, and the run ends. Tap again to restart.
