STORMHAWK

Shooting

STORMHAWK is a vertical arcade shooter with 32 hand-built missions over the Pacific. Fly, dodge with a barrel roll, and chain kills to multiply your score. Land after each mission to spend what you earned in the hangar. Levels: 32 missions across 6 theatres, each ending in its own boss fight, plus mini-bosses along the way. Modes: campaign, endless, a daily mission everyone plays identically, a weekly challenge with mutators, ace mode and a boss rush against all 32 bosses back to back. Planes: 12 unlockable fighters, each with its own weapon pattern and handling — a fast light one, a slow heavy hitter, a three-engine hunter and more. Progres
