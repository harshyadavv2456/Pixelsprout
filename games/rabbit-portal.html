About Rabbit Portal

Rabbit Portal is a fast-paced space-timing puzzle game where precision is everything. In the vastness of outer space, a mysterious swirling portal—nicknamed the Rabbit Portal—opens and closes unpredictably. Your mission: guide incoming spaceships safely into the portal at exactly the right moment. Ships arrive from different directions, speeds, and patterns. Tap or drag to control their path, avoid collisions, and time their entry perfectly.
