WHOT The Ultimate Nigerian Card Game

Multiplayer

Experience the classic African card game WHOT! Play solo against AI or challenge friends in real-time multiplayer. Features stunning neon graphics, background music, and smooth gameplay. Free to play! GAMEPLAY: 1. On your turn, play a card that matches either: - The SHAPE of the top card (circle, triangle, cross, square, star) - The NUMBER of the top card 2. If you cant play, draw one card from the pile - If the drawn card is playable, you may play it - Otherwise, click SKIP TURN 3. WHOT cards (20) are wild - play them anytime and choose any shape
