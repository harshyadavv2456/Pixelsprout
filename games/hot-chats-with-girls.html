About Hot Chats with Girls

This is an interactive chat story where every message you send affects the relationship. You meet different girls through texting. Each one has her own personality: some are sweet and shy, some are bold and toxic, and some are confident and like to stay in control. Over several days, you will choose replies, follow the heroine’s mood, and decide how to act in the dialogue. Be careful, bold, confident, or too pushy — it all depends on you. The heroines will send messages, photos, test your reactions, flirt, get jealous, and change their attitude toward you based on your choices.
