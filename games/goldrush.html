About GoldRush

GoldRush — a thrilling 2D endless runner where you play as a grumpy little dwarf miner on the hunt for gold! Leap across shaky platforms, grab shiny nuggets, and dodge falling rocks before they crush you flat. Beware of sneaky traps, deadly spikes, and bottomless pits — the cave is out to get you! The deeper you go, the faster the pace. How long can you keep up the rush and prove you’re the toughest miner underground?
