Epic Blocollapse

Puzzle

Enter the world of Epic Block Collapse, where a colorful array of blocks rises steadily. Match them swiftly to prevent a tower from reaching the top. With three unique power-ups at your disposal, clear the blocks en masse and keep the play area clear. Should the blocks touch the upper screen, the game ends. Test your skills and quick thinking in this vibrant block-matching adventure!
