Angry Cockroach

Shooting

Angry Cockroach is a bright 2D physics-destruction game where the kitchen fights back. Pull an equipped cockroach into a handmade launcher, aim, and release it into increasingly fortified cartoon structures. Smash materials, chain combos, trigger close-up special moves, and defeat every animated pest-control guard before the roster runs out. Eighteen raids, ten upgradeable cockroaches, seven limited-use gadgets, daily XP rewards, saved progression, and optional rewarded continues create a replayable campaign on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
