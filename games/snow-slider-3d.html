About Snow Slider 3D

Snow Slider 3D is a fast and fun downhill sledding game where you race through snowy mountains, dodge obstacles, and collect gifts to unlock new sleds. Slide left and right to avoid trees, rocks, snowmen, and other hazards as the speed increases the farther you go. Every run is different thanks to endlessrunner style terrain and smooth 3D graphics.
