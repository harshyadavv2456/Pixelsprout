About African Princesses: Style Island

Today, a fashion show inspired by African traditions will take place on a tropical island. The main stars of the island are the charming Princess Tiara and her friend, the fashionable Princess Zania. Each of them will have to choose the perfect look for the celebration. Change hairstyles, apply makeup and try on beautiful African-style outfits. Complete the look with earrings, sunglasses and traditional-style handbags. Create unforgettable looks for the princesses and discover the magic of Afro-fashion on the Island of Style!
