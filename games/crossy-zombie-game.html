Crossy Zombie Game

Puzzle

Welcome to Crossy Zombie Game, a fast-paced and thrilling survival adventure where danger waits at every step! Enter a world overrun by zombies and test your reflexes, timing, and survival skills as you cross deadly roads, rivers, and zombie-filled zones. In this exciting zombie crossing survival game, your mission is simple but challenging: move forward, avoid obstacles, escape zombies, and survive as long as possible. But be careful — one wrong move can end your run. Zombies attack from all sides, vehicles rush past at high speed, and the environment becomes more dangerous as you progress.
