About Flap Mania: Tap to Survive

Welcome to Flap Mania: Tap to Survive game! Tap to keep your bird in the air, avoid pipes, and survive as long as you can. You can also play Wall Bounce mode, where the bird bounces between the walls and gets faster every time. Collect coins, unlock 20 different birds, beat your best score, and enjoy simple settings like sound, vibration, and revive.
