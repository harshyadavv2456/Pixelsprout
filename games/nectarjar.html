About Nectarjar

Nectarjar is a Pixelsprout original. You drop glowing nectar orbs into a glass jar. Two of the same size that touch fuse into the next form, from dew up to a moonpear. The jar has a spill line. Rest anything above it for too long and the night is over. Chains of fuses are how a high score happens.

How to play

  1. Move the orb along the rim, then drop it into the jar.
  2. Two orbs of the same size fuse when they touch, into the next form.
  3. Bigger forms score more. A fuse that bumps another pair can chain.
  4. Keep the pile under the dashed line. If an orb rests above it, the jar spills.
