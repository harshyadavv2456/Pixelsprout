About Jigsolitaire Deluxe

Jigsolitaire Deluxe is a refined card-based puzzle where every level turns into the creation of a masterpiece. Move picture cards around the board to connect them into a seamless image — from famous paintings like Mona Lisa to serene landscapes and still lifes. As the fragments start to align and the image comes to life, you’ll feel that satisfying moment of completion. Simple controls, smooth gameplay, and visually stunning artworks make this game both relaxing and rewarding.
