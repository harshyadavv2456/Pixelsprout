About Dudu Engineering Truck

DuDu Engineering Truck is a casual and puzzle game where you drive engineering trucks to finish tasks on the worksite. Use your sensitivity to shapes to construct a truck and conquer all challenges ahead. If you love car designing and car games, never miss DuDu Engineering Truck! You will enjoy an adventurous journey of being an engineer.
