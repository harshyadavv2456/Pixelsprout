About Peggle [FLASH]

Peggle is one of the most fun and addictive arcade puzzle games ever created. If you are looking for a game that combines skill, luck, and exciting challenges, then Peggle is the perfect choice for you. Your goal is simple: launch magical balls from the top of the screen and clear all the orange pegs before you run out of shots. Every bounce can lead to an incredible chain reaction, making each level unique and exciting. As you progress through the game, you will unlock powerful characters with special abilities that can help you score even bigger combos and clear difficult stages.
