Labuba Halloween Infestation

Shooting

Monsters are invading Halloween! You must destroy them all — the fate of Halloween is in your hands. The game features three different modes, each with tougher and more powerful monsters. Defeat the monsters and reach the end of the level. But it doesn’t end there — at the end of each stage, you’ll face the strongest and biggest of them all: the Boss Monster. Defeat it to complete the level!The game is playable on both mobile and PC, so you can hunt monsters anytime, anywhere. You can purchase various costumes to change your appearance.
