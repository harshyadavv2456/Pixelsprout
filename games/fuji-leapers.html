About Fuji Leapers

In Fuji Leaper, step into the world of a nimble frog navigating a perilous jungle. Your task? To vanquish swarming bees while racking up points. As you glide through the lush landscape, anticipate increasingly swift and treacherous adversaries. Leap with precision to overcome these obstacles and advance to the next level. But beware, as new threats await—venomous spiders and clouds of poison loom ahead. Hone your reflexes, sharpen your instincts, and discover how far your leaping prowess can take you in this thrilling, action-packed journey!
