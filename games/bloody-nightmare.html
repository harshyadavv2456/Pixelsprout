Bloody Nightmare

Puzzle

The game is intended for players over the age of 18, as in it, you will need to feel like killers and kill live targets from the computer game Schoolboy runaway. - Beautiful graphics and effects. - Addictive gameplay. - 20 levels. When all living targets are destroyed, the level will be completed.
