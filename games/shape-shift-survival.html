Shape Shift Survival

Clicker

Shape Shift Survival is a fast-paced reflex arcade game where players must instantly switch shapes to survive incoming obstacles. Test your reaction speed, focus, and timing as the game gradually becomes faster and more challenging. Every correct move increases your score, while a single mistake ends the run. With smooth controls, responsive gameplay, and clean visuals, Shape Shift Survival is easy to play but hard to master. Perfect for quick sessions on both mobile and desktop devices.
