About Load The Dishes ASMR

Load The Dishes ASMR is a puzzle game that puts you in comfort and fun! Your task is to sort different colored dishes and put them in the dishwasher. By organizing your cutlery in an organized way, you will find that this is not just a simple puzzle challenge, but also a super relaxing ASMR sound experience.
