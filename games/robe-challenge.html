About Robe Challenge

Rope Challenge is an interesting puzzle game where you need to remove rubber bands caught on nails. Each move requires careful calculation to avoid further tangling the system of strings. Observe carefully, choose the right order to remove them, and clear all the strings to complete the level. The gameplay is simple but challenging, providing a relaxing and exciting sense of conquest.
