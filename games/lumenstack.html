About Lumenstack

Lumenstack is a Pixelsprout original. Glowing lumen blocks slide across the night sky. Time your drop so each block lands on the one below. Perfect alignment keeps the full width and multiplies your score. Miss by too much and the stack falls. Chase your best height in this pure timing arcade.

How to play

  1. Tap the screen or press Space / Enter to drop the moving lumen block.
  2. Land it on the tower. The overlapping width becomes the new top.
  3. Perfect drops (almost exact alignment) keep the full size and give bonus points.
  4. Speed rises with score. Your best is saved in this browser.
