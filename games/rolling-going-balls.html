About Rolling Going Balls

Its time to push your reflexes to the limit. Rolling Going Balls is an addictive 3D platform rolling game where you take charge of a fast-moving ball. Rolling and running through tricky tracks full of twists and traps! Smooth rolling controls to guide your ball with precision! Collect as many balls as you can along the way to boost your level! Watch out! Avoid dangerous obstacles like spikes, pendulums, etc.
