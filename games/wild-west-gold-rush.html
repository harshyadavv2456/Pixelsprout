Wild West: Gold Rush

Shooting

Wild West: Gold Rush is a fast-paced arcade reaction shooter where you must defend yourself against bandits appearing in the windows of an old building.Bandits show up unexpectedly and are ready to shoot first. Your goal is to react quickly, shoot the bandits in time, and avoid hitting innocent civilians. With each new level, the difficulty increases: enemies appear faster
