About Bunny Jumpy

Get ready to jump into fun! In Bunny Jumpy, help a super cute pink bunny bounce on stretchy trampolines, dodge tricky obstacles, and soar through the sky! Tap at the right time to keep jumping higher—but be careful: trampolines break after 3 jumps! Simple to play, hard to master, and totally adorable. Features: Colorful 2D cartoon graphics Satisfying bounce mechanics Intuitive one-tap controls Perfect for kids and casual players How far can you jump before the trampoline snaps?
