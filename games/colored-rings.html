Colored Rings

Puzzle

Play Colored Rings game online and for free. In it we invite you to solve a puzzle that will be related to rings. In front of you on the screen you will see a playing field on which there will be rings of different colors. They will be linked to each other. You will need to inspect everything very carefully. Using the mouse, you can rotate these rings in space. By doing this, in the game Colored Rings you will unhook the rings from each other and receive points for this. As soon as you completely clear the field of rings, you will move on to the next level of the game.
