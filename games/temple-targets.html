About Temple Targets

Temple Targets is a mind-bending goal-hitting puzzle game that challenges your planning skills and precision. Your mission: guide your player-object through ancient temple chambers filled with mystical reflectors, calculating the perfect path to hit the hidden targets. Each reflector alters your trajectory by 90 degrees, so careful planning is key to mastering every level.
