Welcome to Little Dentist, the fun and educational game where kids become real dentists! Help your adorable patients by cleaning teeth, fighting cavities, removing plaque, and even adding colorful braces. With friendly characters, silly tools, and cheerful music, children will learn the importance of dental hygiene while having tons of fun.
