SliceStorm

Action

SliceStorm – Fruit Slicing Action Game Slice, dice, and survive in this fast-paced fruit-slicing arcade game! Swipe across your screen to slice juicy fruits while avoiding deadly bombs. Each slice earns points and builds your combo multiplier for massive scores. Features · 7 unique fruits – Watermelon, orange, lemon, apple, pineapple, strawberry, kiwi, and banana (each with special juice effects) · Combo system – Chain slices to multiply your points · 3 lives – One mistake could end your run · Increasing difficulty – Fruits spawn faster as your score rises · Rich 3D-style graphics – Each fruit features detailed cuts and halves · Immersive audio – Satisfying slice sounds and juicy splats How to Play Swipe your finger (or mouse) across fruits to slice them. Miss too many or hit a bomb and you lose a life. Survive as long as possible and climb the leaderboard! Perfect for quick gaming sessions – easy to learn, hard to master. Can you beat your best score?
