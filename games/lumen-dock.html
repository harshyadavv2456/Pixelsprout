About Lumen Dock

Lumen Dock is a Pixelsprout original. A paper lantern slides over a night dock. Drop it so it lands on the lantern below. The overhang is trimmed, the dock rises, and the slide gets faster. A near-perfect drop is a steady and keeps the full width.

How to play

  1. Tap the game, or press Space or Enter, to drop the sliding lantern.
  2. Only the part that overlaps the lantern below stays. A miss tips the stack.
  3. Land almost flush for a steady. Steadies keep the full width and raise the score.
  4. The slide speeds up as the dock grows. Your best score stays in this browser.
