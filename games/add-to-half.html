Add to Half

Puzzle

Sharpen your fraction skills in Add to Half, a fast-paced and brain-teasing math puzzle! In this game, your goal is simple—but tricky: find two fractions that add up to exactly one-half. Each round presents you with a mix of fraction tiles floating or flipping on the screen. Tap or click the correct pair before time runs out! As you progress, the fractions get more challenging — from simple ones like ¼ and ¼ to trickier combinations like and ¹⁄. Speed, accuracy, and quick thinking are your keys to climbing the leaderboard.
