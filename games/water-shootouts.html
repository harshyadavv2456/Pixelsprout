About water shootouts

Water Gun Shooter is an exciting platform game that invites you to embark on an adventure with a powerful water gun. Use your water gun to freeze your enemies, kick them away, or jump on them to collect coins. Move carefully through obstacles and keep an eye on the water level in your pack. You can choose to adventure alone or join up with teammates in thrilling 3v3 battles. Use your earnings to dress up and buy more powerful water guns from the store.
