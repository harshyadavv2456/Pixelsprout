Bricks of Wrath

Shooting

Unleash chaos in Bricks of Wrath — a break-brick game with a fierce twist! Fire powerful shots to destroy the advancing wall of bricks, but stay alert — these aren’t ordinary bricks. Once attacked, they strike back! Dodge incoming fire, time your shots, and survive the relentless onslaught. With every level, the challenge intensifies. It’s not just about breaking bricks — it’s about outlasting their wrath. How long can you hold your ground?
