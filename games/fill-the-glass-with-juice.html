About Fill The Glass With Juice

A bright and calming puzzle game based on realistic fluid physics. Feel like a virtual juice master! Your task is to help the freshly squeezed orange juice find its way to the glass by drawing whimsical paths and channels for it. Enjoy the spectacle as the juicy orange liquid cheerfully spills and flows along the trajectories you created. The levels gradually become more difficult, introducing new obstacles and challenges that will require your wit and precision. Its the perfect game to relax, train your logic, and enjoy the colorful visuals and engaging gameplay. Will you be able to fill the glass to the brim without spilling a single drop
