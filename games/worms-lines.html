Worms Lines

Puzzle

In Worms Lines, youll embark on a fun and challenging puzzle adventure across 80 exciting levels. Your task is to gather all the colored dots and cover every tile with vibrant paint, but be cautious—one wrong move could lead to failure. As you navigate the maze-like environment, youll need to think strategically to reach your goal. Use holes to access new areas, but always plan your steps carefully to avoid getting stuck. Can you conquer every level and complete your painting mission?
