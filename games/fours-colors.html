About fours colors

Fours Colors is a fast-paced and entertaining card game inspired by classic color-matching mechanics. Your objective is simple: be the first player to get rid of all your cards by matching them based on color or number. The game features four main colors—red, blue, green, and yellow—each bringing a vibrant and lively feel to every match. Players must think quickly and act strategically, using special action cards like skip, reverse, and draw to disrupt opponents and gain an advantage. As the game progresses, the tension increases, especially when players are down to their last card and must declare it to avoid penalties.
