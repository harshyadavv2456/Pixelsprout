Happy Corals

Arcade

When the water is too warm, corals expel the algae in their tissue, causing coral bleaching. Global warming and pollution are leading causes of this phenomenon. Unfortunately, bleached corals are more likely to die. The more corals you save, the better. If you enjoyed the game, please dont forget to rate it.
