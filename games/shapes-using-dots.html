Shapes Using Dots

Hypercasual

Shapes Using Dots is a fun and challenging puzzle game that tests your spatial reasoning and problem-solving skills. Your goal is to connect the dots in ascending numerical order to create different shapes. As you progress through the levels, the puzzles become more complex, requiring you to think strategically and plan your moves carefully.
