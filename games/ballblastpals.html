BallBlastPals

Puzzle

BallBlastPals is a bright and exciting bubble-shooter adventure where fun meets fast thinking! Aim your launcher, shoot colorful balls to the top, and match 3 or more of the same type to pop them and score points. Plan your shots carefully, create satisfying chain reactions, and clear the board to win each level. With smooth controls, cheerful visuals, and rewarding gameplay, BallBlastPals is perfect for quick play sessions and endless fun. Game Features: Easy aim-and-shoot mechanics Match 3+ balls to blast and score Colorful, kid-friendly design Fun levels with increasing challenge Satisfying combos and chain reactions
