About Dewrack

Dewrack is a Pixelsprout original. A night garden sheds dew onto a leaf rack. Slide the rack to catch each drop. Two drops of the same size that touch fuse into the next form, from bead to pearl, orb, lantern, moon, and tide. A drop that slips past the rack costs a life. Three misses end the run.

How to play

  1. Slide the leaf rack with a finger, or press Left and Right, so falling dew lands on it.
  2. Drops of the same size that touch fuse into the next form and add a chain bonus.
  3. Keep the rack under the pile. A drop that rolls off the ends falls and costs a life.
  4. You have three lives. The best score stays in this browser.
