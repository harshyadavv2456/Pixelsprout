About Nine Cards Of Winter

Get into the holiday spirit with Nine Cards of Winter, a fun and relaxing tile-matching game! Pick up to 9 Christmas tiles and match 3 of the same to clear them. Watch out — if your stack fills up, the game is over! Use smart moves to uncover hidden tiles, solve tricky puzzles, and enjoy cute holiday designs.It’s easy to play, relaxing to enjoy, and full of winter charm!
