About Crystal Craash

Crystal Crush is an addictive gem merge puzzle! Tap groups of 3 or more matching gems to merge them into a new gem with double the value. Chain combos, plan your moves carefully and keep the board alive as long as possible. Use power-ups like Thunder, Bomb and Shuffle to escape tight situations. How high can you score? Simple to learn, hard to master — endless fun for everyone!
