About Ricochet Arena

Ricochet Arena is a high-tension arcade shooter where every shot counts. Trapped inside a sealed arena with your targets, bullets ricochet off walls, turning precision into survival. Eliminate all enemies using smart angles and calculated rebounds but beware: one careless shot can come back to end you. Think fast, aim sharper, and master the bounce.
