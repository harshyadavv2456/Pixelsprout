About Jetspire

Jetspire is a fast-paced arcade game where you guide a jetpack-equipped runner upward through an endless vertical challenge. Pass through moving gates that open and close as you climb higher, earning a point for every screen you clear. As you ascend, the gates move faster, testing your timing and reflexes. Stay focused, react quickly, and see how high you can go.
