Toxic Drip

Puzzle

Toxic Drip is an eerie and thrilling match-3 puzzle game set in a dark and spooky world. Players must match toxic-themed items, such as skulls, eyeballs, and pumpkins, to clear the board and score points. As the levels progress, the toxic drip gets faster and the puzzles become more challenging, making each new stage an exciting test of your skills. With creepy visuals, eerie sound effects, and intuitive gameplay, Toxic Drip provides an engaging experience for players who love spooky games.
