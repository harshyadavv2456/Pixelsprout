Crown Crusher

Hypercasual

Crown Crusher – Smash, Collect, and Conquer! Step into the royal adventure of Crown Crusher, an exciting action-packed game where a powerful lady queen wields her mighty hammer to smash everything in her path and collect as much gold as possible! As the queen moves forward, your mission is to crush obstacles, break treasures, and gather sparkling gold coins scattered along the way. But beware — the journey is not without danger. Zombies lurk ahead, ready to stop the queen’s quest for riches. You are given three lives, but every time the queen encounters a zombie, she loses one life. If she meets three zombies in total, the adventure end
