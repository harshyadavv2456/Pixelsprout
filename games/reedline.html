About Reedline

Reedline is a Pixelsprout original. A lantern hangs from a reed over a night pond. It swings. Release at the right moment and it arcs to the next reed. Catch the hook near its center and the chain climbs. Miss the reed and the lantern drops into the water.

How to play

  1. Tap, or press Space, to start from the title screen.
  2. Watch the lantern swing. Tap again to let go.
  3. Land on the next reed. A catch near the center is a perfect and starts a chain.
  4. Miss, or fly past the pond, and the run ends. Tap to swing again. Your best stays in this browser.
