The Forest Friends

Puzzle

Step into a magical woodland adventure in The Forest Friends! Match colorful forest items like berries, leaves, mushrooms, and more to complete each level’s goals. With a limited number of moves, every match counts, plan carefully to collect all the required items before you run out. As you progress, levels become more challenging with new layouts and objectives to master. Easy to play but full of strategy, The Forest Friends offers a relaxing yet rewarding puzzle experience. Can you gather everything the forest needs and help your friends thrive?
