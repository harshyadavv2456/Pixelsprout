Crazy 3

Stickman

Crazy Three is a unique puzzle game that combines stickman fights, battles, and wars with physics-based mechanics, where all objects are affected by gravity. With over 200 challenging levels and multiple solutions for each puzzle, the game caters to different playstyles. Featuring stunning cartoon graphics, It’s a fun way to improve your problem-solving and stickman drawing skills. Play it now and start your stickman adventure!
