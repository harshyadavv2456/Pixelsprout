Forest Wing

Arcade

Forest Wing is a hyper-casual butterfly flying game. Guide your butterfly through ancient forest tree trunks, collect golden seeds, and survive as long as possible! Features 5 unlockable butterfly skins, Classic and Lightning (30-second sprint) modes, XP progression with 11 levels, 7-day daily streak bonuses, 8 achievements, seasonal visual themes (Halloween, Winter, Spring, Summer, Forest), and an interactive tutorial for new players.
