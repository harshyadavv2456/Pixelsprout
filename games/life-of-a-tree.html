Embrace the tranquil journey of nurturing nature in Life of a Tree. Plant seeds, tend to saplings, and guide them to majestic glory. With interactive learning through quizzes and mini-games, discover the intricate beauty of tree ecology while protecting your grove from challenges. A delightful blend of fun and education awaits!
