Big Supermarket Simulator

Hypercasual

Big Supermarket Simulator is a light business simulation game: start from a small supermarket, restock, cashier, clean, and add new products. Every gold coin you earn can be invested in upgrades. As the scale expands, you can open coffee shops and flower shops to create a multi-business commercial street. Hire employees, unlock automated production lines, optimize movement lines and warehousing, and improve customer satisfaction and unit price. Even if you take a break, offline income will continue to accumulate - step by step, turn your store into the most lively shopping landmark in the city!
