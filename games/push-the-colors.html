The color push begins with colorful squares, obstacles, and color gates within a great 2 player casual game! Find various costumes during the Push the Colors adventure, get the cubes to the finish line, and challenge the giant at the end! The more cubes you have, the more power you will have. You can destroy the color monster by having many cubes. Make smart choices on your way and increase the number of the cubes in your hands.
