Toxic Arena: Biological Threat

Shooting

Toxic Arena: Biological Threat is a thrilling action-roguelike survivors game where your only goal is to survive in the heart of a global biological catastrophe. You are a fighter trapped on an arena with endless hordes of mutants. Test your nerves and your reaction time! What awaits you: Insane Drive: Fight against ever-growing waves of enemies. Every minute on the arena is a new challenge and a test of your tactics. Deep Mutation System: Choose unique upgrades with each level! Increase damage, fire rate, or unlock abilities like ricochet and armor penetration.
