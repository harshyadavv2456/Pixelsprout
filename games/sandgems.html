SandGems

Hypercasual

SandGems is an exciting desert adventure game where a brave Sultan journeys across the scorching sands to collect precious gems. But the desert is far from safe — lethal bugs and crawling creatures lurk everywhere, ready to stop your quest. Guide the Sultan through the desert, gather as many shining gems as possible, and carefully avoid the dangerous bugs roaming the sands. Quick reflexes and smart movement are the key to surviving longer and collecting greater treasure. The more gems you collect, the greater your score. But beware — the desert grows more dangerous the longer you stay!
