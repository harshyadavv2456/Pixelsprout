About Zappy

Zappy is a fun 2D puzzle adventure where you control a clever robot on a mission to deliver a mysterious box to its destination. Navigate tricky obstacles, avoid dangerous traps, outsmart enemies, and solve challenging puzzles to complete each level. Use strategy and precise movement to protect the box and reach the goal. With engaging gameplay, creative levels, and smooth controls, Zappy offers an exciting adventure for puzzle game fans.
