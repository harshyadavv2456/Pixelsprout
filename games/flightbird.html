FlightBird

Hypercasual

Get ready for a fast-paced flying challenge! In Flight Bird, your mission is simple: tap or click to keep your bird in the air and dodge the tricky obstacles. Sounds easy? Think again! This endless arcade game will test your reflexes, timing, and focus. Designed with smooth controls and vibrant visuals, Flight Bird offers a classic gameplay experience thats easy to pick up but hard to master. How far can you fly without crashing? Perfect for quick gaming sessions, Flight Bird is lightweight, addictively fun, and suitable for all ages. Compete with friends, unlock new birds, and aim for the highest score!
