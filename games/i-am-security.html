I Am Security

Hypercasual

Have you ever wanted to be a hardened security officer and be on the front lines of maintaining order? In I Am Security, an ultra-realistic role-playing simulator, you play the role of a professional security guard. You decide whether or not to allow them to enter. Some guests may try to sneak in with prohibited items and find these hidden items. Performs tasks in a variety of scenarios to identify suspicious persons.
