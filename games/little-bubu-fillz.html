About Little Bubu Fillz

Little Bubu Fillz is a relaxing yet challenging level puzzle game where every move matters! Meet Bubu, the adorable bunny who loves solving puzzles. Your task is simple—swipe to fill every block on the board. But don’t be fooled by the cute looks; as levels progress, puzzles become trickier and require more thinking. You must plan your swipes carefully to cover every tile without missing any. With its colorful design, cheerful sound, and smooth gameplay, Little Bubu Fillz is perfect for all ages. Sharpen your brain and enjoy hours of calm, fun puzzle-solving with Bubu!
