About Taipei GTA

Unofficial fan/parody game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
Starts in Chinese; use the EN button at the top of the title screen to switch to English.
An open-world city game set in Taipei, called Taipei Rush on its title screen. The title menu has Start Game, Switch city, Character, Settings and Controls, and you can play as a guest.
Game by @aicodewithme. It is played on the creator’s site, taipei-gta.vercel.app; Pixelsprout only links to it. All rights belong to the creator.
