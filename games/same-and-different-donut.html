Same and Different Donut

Puzzle

Get ready to have some fun with the game Same & Different Donut! Take a close look at these delicious, colorful donuts. Can you spot which ones look exactly the same? Now, see if you can find the one that’s different from the rest. You’ll get to answer a few fun questions along the way. After that, it’s time to get creative and design your very own donut—choose your favorite colors and toppings! And of course, don’t forget to pretend to take a big bite and “eat” your creation. Enjoy the game and have lots of fun!
