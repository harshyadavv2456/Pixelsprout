About Never Fall Pinball

Never Fall Pinball is a normal pinball game where pins are made extra large so that the balll usually fall unless there are some exceptional cases. You have to collect stars but hitting a black star ruins it and game is over. This is for kids who want an easier pinball game! Have fun :)
