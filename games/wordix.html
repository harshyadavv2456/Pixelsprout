Wordix

Puzzle

Wordix is an engaging logic game where your task is to guess the word within a limited number of attempts. It’s the Russian version of the popular game Wordle, but with one important difference: the words are chosen from a pre-prepared list, and you can play as much as you want without waiting for a new word each day. The game develops your thinking skills, expands your vocabulary, and provides an entertaining way to spend time. It’s suitable for both children and adults. Simple rules make the game accessible to everyone, and color-coded hints help you find the correct answer faster.
