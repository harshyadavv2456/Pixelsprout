Gothic Knife

Hypercasual

Test your reflexes and precision in this addictive knife throwing game! Your goal is simple: throw knives into the rotating logs to break them. Be careful not to hit the knives youve already thrown! Key Features: • Challenge yourself with endless levels. • Battle unique Bosses every 3 stages! • Explore 3 stunning themes: Cyberpunk, Gothic, and Village. • Collect bonuses to earn extra knives. • Master the Combo system for massive high scores!
