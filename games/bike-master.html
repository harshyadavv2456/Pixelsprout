Bike Master

Hypercasual

Bike Master is a physics-based puzzle game where you build the missing parts of the track and ride your way to the finish line! Draw and assemble platforms, ramps, and supports, then pedal your bike across tricky terrain without crashing. Each level is a fresh challenge: balance speed and caution, handle gaps and obstacles, and find the smartest path through. With a Time Mode for speedrunners and dozens of brain-teasing levels, Bike Master rewards both careful planners and bold riders. Simple to learn, hard to master. Can you complete every level and become the true Bike Master?
