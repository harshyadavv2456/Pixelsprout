Robber and cop

Action

Become a ruthless bandit in the exciting game Robber and cop! Rob, and steal - all the ways are good to get rich! You are in the role of a daring bandit who wants to get rich. You have audacity, ingenuity and a faithful bat in your arsenal! Earn money: - Beat money out of passers-by using your power. - Rob the bank by defeating the guard and taking the money with you. - Rob houses, taking valuable furniture with you. - Steal cars. Develop your character: - Buy new weapons, armor and other upgrades to become Become a legend: - Go from a petty thief to a formidable mafia boss whose name inspires fear.
