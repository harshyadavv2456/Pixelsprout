Meet The Birds

Baby Hazel

Meet the Birds is an engaging and educational game designed for children to learn about different bird species in a fun and interactive way. The game features a vibrant and colorful environment where players can explore various habitats and meet a variety of birds. Each bird introduces itself with interesting facts and playful animations, making learning enjoyable and memorable. The game aims to enhance childrens knowledge about birds, their sounds, and their habitats while encouraging curiosity and exploration.
