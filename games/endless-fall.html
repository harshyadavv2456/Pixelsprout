Endless Fall

Puzzle

Welcome to Endless Fall, the ultimate test of reflex and precision! Your goal is simple — guide the ball safely as it falls endlessly downward. But the path is filled with tricky obstacles that you must move, rotate, and position just right to clear the way. Each level gets faster and more challenging, demanding perfect timing and smart thinking. How long can you keep falling?
