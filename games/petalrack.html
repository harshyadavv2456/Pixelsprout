About Petalrack

Petalrack is a Pixelsprout original. A night rack holds an eight-by-eight bed. You set blossom clusters into empty soil. A full row or a full column harvests at once, and a second line in the same drop multiplies the score. The tray shows three clusters. When none of them fit, the bed is full.

How to play

  1. Tap a cluster in the tray, or press 1, 2, or 3, to hold it.
  2. Tap a soil cell, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter, to set that cluster down.
  3. Fill a whole row or column and it harvests. Two lines in one drop score more.
  4. Space or Enter starts a rack from the title screen and starts again after the bed fills. Your best stays in this browser.
