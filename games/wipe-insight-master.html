Wipe Insight Master

Hypercasual

Wipe Insight Master is a free drawing online game for boys. Use your finger to draw and outline the shapes of the parts that the drawings need. Solve puzzles by using your powers of logic and lateral thinking as you work your way through a huge series of cute drawings that all have something a little bit wrong with them, its up to you to work it out!
