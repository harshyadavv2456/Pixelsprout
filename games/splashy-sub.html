Splashy Sub

Adventure

Dive deep into the ocean in Splashy Sub, a fun and challenging underwater adventure! Control your tiny yellow submarine and navigate through dangerous underwater caves filled with obstacles, mines, and sea creatures. Tap or press the right keys to stay afloat — every move counts! Avoid crashing, collect coins, and survive as long as you can beneath the waves. Easy to play but hard to master, Splashy Sub combines simple controls with addictive arcade action. How far can you go before the ocean swallows you whole? Features: Flappy-style underwater gameplay Simple one-touch or keyboard control
