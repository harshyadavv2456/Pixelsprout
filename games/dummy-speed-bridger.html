About Dummy Speed Bridger

Dummy Speed Bridge is a fast-paced reflex game where timing is everything. Dash forward as bridges snap into place beneath your feet, then vanish just as quickly. One wrong step and it’s a long fall down. Test your reaction speed, memory, and nerves as the pace ramps up with every level. Simple controls, brutal momentum, and zero mercy—only the quickest dummies make it across.
