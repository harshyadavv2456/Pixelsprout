Spirit Drops From Dew

Arcade

Catch the falling lights, evolve the spirit, and embrace the magic of the forest. In Spirit Drops, you play as a mystical being catching drops of light falling from the sky. Each drop brings you closer to your next evolution—from a tiny glowing orb to a majestic spirit of light. But beware! Not all that falls is pure. Dark orbs must be avoided at all costs. You have 60 seconds. Catch light drops to earn points. Watch your spirit evolve with each milestone. Miss a dark orb and face the consequences... Simple controls: Swipe or use Arrow keys to move. How far can your spirit grow?
