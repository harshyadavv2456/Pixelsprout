Musical Labubu: Make a Melody

Clicker

Musical Labubu - Make a Melody is a game about creating your own melody in a small 3D world. Here youll see a variety of Labubu, with a variety of animations and sounds. The game features a night mode that can really surprise and scare you. If youre not afraid, youll love it. Come on in and create your favorite melody!
