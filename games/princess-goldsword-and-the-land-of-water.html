Princess Goldsword and The Land of Water

Clicker

In a world of magic in a far away land, there is an island where Trinks lives a very friendly creature, Princess Goldsword. They have the loyalty of the courageous Princess. It protects from all dangers. But something terrible has happened! The monsters from the waters stole all the treasures of Trinks. And worse, stolen children also! The task is to help Princess Goldsword recover the stolen treasures while facing monsters, traps, and challenges of all sizes.
