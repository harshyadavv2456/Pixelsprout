About Torn Pieces

Torn Pieces s a simple and fun jigsaw puzzle game. Place the broken pieces in the correct spots to complete the picture. Drag and drop each piece carefully and watch the image come together. If you get stuck, use the hint feature—it will place one random piece in the correct position to help you move forward.
