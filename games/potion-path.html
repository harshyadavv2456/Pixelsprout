Potion Path

Adventure

Welcome to Potion Path – A Magical Running Adventure! Join a playful young witch on a thrilling journey through enchanted lands filled with danger, mystery, and magical rewards. In Potion Path, your mission is to run, jump, and collect powerful elements and tasty treats, all while dodging deadly enemies lurking on the path! How to Play: Tap to jump over obstacles and evil creatures. Collect magical food and potion ingredients to boost your score. Use skill and timing to survive as long as you can — lives are limited, so every jump counts!
