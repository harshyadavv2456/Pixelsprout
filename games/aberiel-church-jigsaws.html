About Aberiel Church Jigsaws

Escape the noise of daily life and embark on a peaceful journey through the world’s most beautiful churches in Aberiel Church Jigsaw Puzzles Saga — Chapter 1. Explore stunning churches from around the world while solving relaxing jigsaw puzzles. Choose from 12, 20, or 42 pieces and rebuild each image at your own pace. Created with carefully retouched artwork, the game combines relaxing puzzle gameplay with a worldwide architectural journey. Complete each church to unlock the next and continue your journey from one remarkable place of worship to another.
