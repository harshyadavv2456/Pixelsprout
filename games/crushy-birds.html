About Crushy Birds

ChatGPT Crushy Birds is an engaging and fast-paced browser game where players must eliminate birds by clicking on them as they fly across the screen. The game features colorful, cartoonish graphics that appeal to players of all ages. Each successful click scores points, with the game increasing in difficulty as more birds appear and fly faster. Strategic clicks and sharp reflexes are essential to achieve high scores in this addictive and fun-filled challenge.
