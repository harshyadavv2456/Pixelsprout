Eggstreme Eggscape

Arcade

Eggstreme Eggscape is an arcade game where you play as an egg that needs to escape from the hot lava. Every jump is a leap for survival, so make every jump count! Escape the sizzling river of lava underneath you. Climb and jump up the wall with care and precision. Collect coins and diamonds as you ascend the treacherous path. The lava will speed up on each level, so be fast, or you will get fried. Will you rise as the champion or boil in defeat?
