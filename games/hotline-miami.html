About Hotline Miami

Hotline Miami is an exciting 2D top-down shooter. Players are immersed in a vibrant, retro world with fast-paced, intense combat where every mistake can be fatal. Youll encounter a variety of levels with enemies that require a strategic approach, a wide range of weapons, and high stakes where every second counts. Pixel graphics and an 80s-inspired neon atmosphere create a unique aesthetic, while intense firefights and epic soundtracks immerse you in an atmosphere of danger and adrenaline.
