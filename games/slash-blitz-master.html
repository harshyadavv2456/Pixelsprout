Slash Blitz Master

Hypercasual

A hyper-addictive casual action game: Grab your blade, aim, and slash wacky rubber foes! Dodge their weapons, fling your knife to take them down, and unlock deadlier blades. Race through vibrant stages—tropical beaches, crate docks—with a looming helicopter (reward or threat?). Level up to become the Slash Master: collect upgrades, crush tough enemies, and savor one-hit thrills. Simple taps, nonstop chaos, quick, satisfying fun. Can you blitz every wave and claim the title?
