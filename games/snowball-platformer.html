SnowBall: Platformer

Puzzle

SnowBall: Platformer is a joyful physics-based platformer made for the holidays! You play as SnowBall — a lively snowball on a mission to collect every gift and make New Year’s Eve happen! Roll, bounce, and roll again through 25 cleverly designed levels, each ending with a special finish-line gift. Without it… there’s no New Year! The game starts easy, but don’t be fooled — challenges grow with every level! Master momentum, timing, and realistic physics to succeed. Help SnowBall gather all the gifts before midnight strikes… or the holiday will vanish!
