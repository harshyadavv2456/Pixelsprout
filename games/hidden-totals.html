Hidden Totals

Hypercasual

Hidden Totals is a fun and engaging counting game where you test your observation skills by finding how many times a specific object appears in a given image! Look closely, count carefully, and tap the correct total to score points. With increasing difficulty across multiple levels, this game challenges your focus, memory, and quick thinking. Perfect for kids and adults alike, Hidden Totals is a great way to sharpen your counting skills while having fun. Can you spot them all? Play now and put your eyes to the test!
