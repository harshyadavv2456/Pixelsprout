Radiation Zone

Shooting

It is FPS shooter with zombies and military. Your mission is to clear area from all terrorists on the restricted radiation zone. You can find and use many kind of weapons and amo on the area. Available for You inventory, where you can collect a loot for surving. For make a tent, you need to collect a four clothes and four branches. Also You need to find a water and food for a life. Good luck for complex mission!
