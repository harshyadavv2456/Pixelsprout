About Paint over the lines

It is not always easy to paint over the lines, the number of runners and the geometry of the lines are constantly changing. Extreme care is needed to make everything work out for you. Each little man can only run along his own path and paint it with a certain color. You need to paint over all the lines and avoid any collisions.
