About Moonbloom

Moonbloom is a Pixelsprout original. Drop seeds into a night planter. Three or more of the same plant touching by an edge grow into the next form, and chains multiply the score. Pebbles block a cell until a neighboring merge cracks them, or a moonbloom shatters them. Rain upgrades the top plant in a column. Clear five evenings, then chase your best in the monsoon. Tap a column, or use the arrow keys and Enter. Nothing to download.
