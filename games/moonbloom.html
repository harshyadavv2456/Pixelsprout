About Moonbloom

Moonbloom is a Pixelsprout original. Drop seeds into a night planter. Three or more of the same plant touching by an edge grow into the next form, and chains multiply the score. Pebbles block a cell until a neighboring merge cracks them, or a moonbloom shatters them. Rain upgrades the top plant in a column. Clear five evenings, then chase your best in the monsoon.

How to play

  1. Tap a column, or press Left and Right, then Enter, to drop the piece in your hand.
  2. Three or more of the same plant touching by an edge grow into the next form. Chains score more.
  3. Pebbles block a cell until a neighboring merge cracks them. A moonbloom shatters them.
  4. Rain upgrades the top plant in that column. Clear five evenings, then keep going for a high score.
