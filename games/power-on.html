Power On

Puzzle

n Power On, a demanding HTML5 skill game, your objective is to illuminate every bulb in the least amount of time possible. To do this, you must correctly connect all the wires to the power supply. The difficulty of the game increases as you maneuver through intricate wire systems and make quick choices to turn on all the bulbs. To excel in Power On, strategize your moves thoughtfully and apply strategic thinking for optimal performance. Strive for minimal wire rotations to conserve time. Remember, not every bulb will have a direct connection to the power supply. Utilize junctions and intersections effectively.
