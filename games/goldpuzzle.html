GoldPuzzle

Puzzle

A sparkling gold puzzle game that collects gold by eliminating gold nugets. Drag gold to fill the line or column, eliminate and collect gold, you can collect gold, pin gold and purple gold, their prices are different, when can not continue can consume gold replacement gold nugget, can also watch the advertisement to get a new gold nugget, each elimination reaches a certain amount will have an additional reward
