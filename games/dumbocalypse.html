Guide the future of humanity in Dumbocalypse, where your strategic answers on a critical app determine survival. Balance wisdom and whimsy to navigate everyday dilemmas caused by Professor Brain's experiment gone awry, turning ordinary folks profoundly dim. Your responses forge a finely balanced world, influencing joy, population, and existence itself.
