The Garbaggio Hotel

Hypercasual

Welcome to Garbaggio Hotel, where guest comfort takes a backseat to cold, hard cash! Tap to squeeze every penny from your unfortunate visitors, then invest in expanding your sprawling tower of mediocrity. Build new floors, unlock hilarious upgrades, and watch animated guests wander through your establishment with varying degrees of regret. Automate your money-making empire and watch profits soar even when youre away. Accumulate enough wealth to trigger Renovation Credits – a prestige system that makes your terrible hotel slightly less terrible (but way more profitable). Big number progression, strategic upgrades, and idle gameplay mechanics.
