Retro Santa

Clicker

In this exciting game, Santa Claus runs and jumps to recover the lost gifts from his sack. Along the way, two enemies throw stones at him, which Santa must skillfully avoid. The goal is to survive as long as possible while collecting all the fallen gifts. Occasionally, eating a fruit will restore some of Santas energy, helping him to continue his journey. The faster you act, the more gifts you collect, but beware of the enemies attacks! Can you help Santa recover all the presents and save Christmas?
