Romantic K Pop Transformation

Girls

In this game, the stars of K-pop are preparing for a romantic transformation. Choose a girl and create a unique image for her, full of tenderness and brilliance of the stage. Experiment with makeup in red and pink tones; change the hairstyles and hair color of the girls. Choose the perfect outfit: from cute dresses and stylish tops to trendy skirts and trousers. Complete the look with handbags, jewelry, and fashion accessories. Compare the resulting images and decide which of the heroines of the game has become the real queen of romantic K-pop style.
