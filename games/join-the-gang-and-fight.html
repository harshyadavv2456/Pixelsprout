Join the Gang and Fight

Adventure

Join the Gang and Fight is a hilarious online multiplayer physics-based PVP game where stickman warriors battle it out in wobbly ragdoll arenas! Invite your friends to form a gang and compete in slapstick fights using punches, kicks, and crazy ragdoll moves. Play fun events like Brawl, Kick the King, Steal the Chicken, Racing, and Football, all with totally accurate physics that make each fight unpredictable and hilarious. Master your stickman, knock out opponents, and enjoy endless multiplayer chaos in this wobbly, action-packed party game!
