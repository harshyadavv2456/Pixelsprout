Worm And Apple

Puzzle

Worm And Apple is a fun puzzle game where you control a worm to navigate through terrains to eat delicious apples. Plan your path carefully, avoid obstacles, and use the environment to complete each level. The difficulty gradually increases with new challenges, requiring logical thinking and dexterity. The gameplay is simple yet addictive, perfect for puzzle and casual game enthusiasts!
