About Chinchilla Trails

Chinchilla Trails is a cozy route-building puzzle about helping a lost chinchilla find the way home. Rotate each trail tile until the board becomes one continuous route from the chinchilla to the house. Collect every snack along the way and aim for the perfect-turn target to earn all three stars. The target is a challenge rather than a move limit, so players can keep experimenting for as long as they need. The journey contains 100 increasingly challenging stages across five colorful worlds, a complete animated tutorial, calm procedural music, playful sound effects, local progress saving and responsive mouse or touch controls.
