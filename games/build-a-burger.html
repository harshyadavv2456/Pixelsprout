Build a Burger

Hypercasual

Get ready for burger madness in the game Build a Burger! Run forward, collect ingredients according to the recipe, and upgrade your burger, making it bigger and tastier. Fun obstacles await you along the way—be quick and attentive to make sure you dont miss a single ingredient! Collect ingredients, experiment with recipes, and upgrade your burger to incredible sizes. Each new dish unlocks unique opportunities and bonuses, and finally, the most important thing: feed your giant burger and enjoy the result! Fun, bright, and dynamic, this runner will bring a wave of emotions to both children and adults. Simple controls, engaging levels, fun
