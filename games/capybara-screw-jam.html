Welcome to Capybara Screw Jam! This is a cute and brain-teasing puzzle game. You’ll join a lovable capybara on a journey of problem-solving and strategy. With simple gameplay and increasing challenges, it’s the perfect game for anyone who loves casual puzzles and brain workouts. Your goal is to help the capybara solve various puzzle challenges. Each level presents various colors of capybara.
