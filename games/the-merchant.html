About The Merchant

Set sail in a vibrant pixelated world where the sands of time meet the seas, in The Merchant. Venture to ancient desert ports and immerse yourself in trading intrigue as you seek fortune and fame. Navigate the yesteryears bustling marketplaces, fulfilling quests set by the governor, all to upgrade your trusty ship. Its a captivating blend of adventure and commerce, and your choices dictate your path. But beware, financial woe could leave you bankrupt. Are you ready to embrace the mercantile life and uncover the treasures of the desert?
