About Spookily Stylish Duo

Two friends, Arelia and Tiara, are looking forward to autumn to meet her fully armed in style! Join the girls: try on fashionable autumn looks and choose accessories that will emphasize the character of each heroine. But do not limit yourself to the wardrobe! Light up the lights at the house, decorate the entrance and turn the courtyard into a cozy corner of a fairy tale. Let your imagination run wild and create a truly terrifyingly stylish duo!
