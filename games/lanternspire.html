About Lanternspire

Lanternspire is a Pixelsprout original. A festival lantern swings over a wooden stand. Drop it so it lands on the lantern below. A clean overlap keeps the spire wide. A near miss trims the lantern. A full miss ends the night. Perfect drops light a spark combo and the swing gets faster as the tower grows.

How to play

  1. Tap the canvas, or press Space or Enter, to drop the swinging lantern.
  2. Land it on the lantern below. The overlap becomes the width of the next piece.
  3. A drop within a few pixels of center is a spark. Sparks keep the full width and raise the combo.
  4. Left and Right, or A and D, nudge the swing before you drop. R starts a new spire.
