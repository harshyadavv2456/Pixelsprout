About Bad Cat Prankster Moms Return

Play as the naughtiest cat in town in Bad Cat Prankster - Moms Return! Step into the paws of a mischievous feline and turn an ordinary house into your personal playground. Scratch furniture, knock objects off shelves, climb onto rooftops, and create hilarious chaos before Mom catches you. Explore every corner of the house, complete funny prank missions, and use your cat instincts to outsmart humans. But be careful Mom is always watching, and youll need to stay sneaky to avoid getting caught. Hide, escape, and continue your playful adventures while causing maximum mischief.
