About Bir In a Pot

Play at Bird In A Pot and getinto a spooky, fun-filled puzzle adventure set in a Darktoon atmosphere! Your goal is to guide the little green bird into the magic pot without letting it touch the ground. Complete all 24 challenging levels filled with tricky obstacles and surprises! How do you do it? Simply destroy the wooden boxes strategically and use the principles of physics to roll the pumpkins into the pot. With its vibrant Dark-themed graphics and addictive gameplay, its a simple yet captivating puzzle game that will delight players of all ages.
