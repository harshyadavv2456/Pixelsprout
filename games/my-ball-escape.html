About My Ball Escape

My Ball Escape is a fast and fun challenge that will keep your heart racing and your fingers ready. Trapped inside spinning orbit shapes, your ball is desperate to break free and only your timing can set it loose. Tap at the perfect moment to launch the ball through rotating barriers, dodge closing gaps, and escape the orbit before it’s too late.
