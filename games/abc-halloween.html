About ABC Halloween

ABC Halloween is a delightful educational game for kids ages 2-6, combining alphabet learning with Halloween fun! Children explore a magical world filled with friendly ghosts, pumpkins, and witches, learning letters through interactive visuals and exciting mini-games.
