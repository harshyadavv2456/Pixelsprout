Noob the builder

Clicker

Noob the builder is a fun clicker game inspired by Minecraft. Help the noob build his house , earn coins, and unlock new levels. Increase your click power. The engaging gameplay and visible progress with each click make this game a great choice for all Minecraft fans. Start your adventure and see how far you can go!
