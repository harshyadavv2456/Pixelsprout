How to actually pick the right gift

Most gift guides are just long lists sorted by price, which is why they rarely help when you're stuck. The four things that actually narrow a gift down are who it's for, why you're giving it, what you're willing to spend, and what they're genuinely into - in that order. Gift File asks exactly those four questions, nothing else, and scores a running dataset of gift ideas against your answers instead of showing you the same generic top-10 list everyone else gets.

It also corrects itself. If none of the three suggestions are right, tell it what you actually ended up getting - that gets folded back into the dataset with a small weight, so the next person with a similar answer set sees a slightly better set of ideas. Nothing here is AI-generated; it's a transparent weighted match between your answers and a hand-seeded (and visitor-taught) list of gifts, so results stay specific instead of vague.

India or elsewhere?

Gift File asks upfront rather than guessing from your IP address, then routes every "Search on Amazon" link to amazon.in or amazon.com accordingly, so the results are actually shoppable wherever you are.
