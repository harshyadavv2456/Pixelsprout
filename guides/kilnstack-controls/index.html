Kilnstack - Controls

KilnstackArcade · Free to play
Play now
Advertisement

Touch

Tap anywhere on the game to set the swinging brick. On the title screen, the first tap starts the kiln. After a fall, tap to start again. The game is built for a phone held upright.

Keyboard

Space or Enter sets the brick, starts a run, and restarts after a fall. R starts a fresh run while you are playing.

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button under the title to fill the screen. Press Esc to leave it.
