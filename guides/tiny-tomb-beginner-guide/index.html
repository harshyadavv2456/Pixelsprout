Tiny Tomb Beginner's Guide - How to Get Started

Guide · Pixel

New to Tiny Tomb? This covers what you need to know before your first playthrough.

What is Tiny Tomb?

Tiny Tomb is a pixel game you can play instantly in your browser, no download required.

First steps

  1. Start the game and get familiar with the main screen.
  2. Play through the first level or round without worrying about your score.
  3. Check the in-game instructions if you're unsure what to do next.

Who it's for

Fans of pixel games looking for something quick to play.
