Dewrack - Controls

DewrackArcade · Free to play
Play now
Advertisement

Touch

Press and drag on the garden. The leaf rack follows your finger. Tap the title or the end screen to start a run. The game is built for a phone held upright.

Keyboard

Left and Right, or A and D, slide the rack. Space or Enter starts a run and restarts after the rack overflows.

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button under the title to fill the screen. Press Esc to leave it.
