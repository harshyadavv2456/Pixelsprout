Super Retro Ping-Pong Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Super Retro Ping-Pong is a classic arcade-style game where players control a paddle to hit a ball back and forth on a simple court.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can likely use the mouse to move the paddle left and right, or possibly the arrow keys on the keyboard for more precise control.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, touch controls probably involve tapping or swiping on the screen to move the paddle, with the direction and speed of the swipe possibly affecting the paddle's movement.

Tips

  • Timing is crucial, so practice your reactions to hit the ball at the right moment.
  • Control the speed and direction of your paddle carefully to catch tricky shots.
  • Pay attention to the ball's trajectory to anticipate where it will bounce
