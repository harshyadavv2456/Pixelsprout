What you are doing

Dew falls on a leaf rack in a night garden. You slide the rack so drops land, then fuse matching sizes before the pile spills.

A catch

A drop that hits the rack settles on it and drifts toward the middle. Two drops of the same size that touch become the next form. The new drop can immediately fuse again, and a chain multiplies the score.

Forms

Bead, pearl, orb, lantern, moon, then tide. Only equal sizes fuse. A tide drop is the top of the chain and does not fuse further.

Lives

You start with three. A drop that misses the rack, or rolls off either end, falls out of the garden and costs a life. The run ends at zero. Your best score stays in this browser.
