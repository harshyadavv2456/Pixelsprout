What you are doing

You fill an eight-by-eight garden rack with blossom clusters. A full row or a full column harvests and clears. The rack ends when none of the clusters in the tray can sit on empty soil.

A turn

The tray under the bed holds up to three clusters. Hold one, then set it on empty cells. The ghost shows where it will land. A cluster never covers a blossom that is already planted.

Harvests

After a drop, every full row and every full column clears. One line is a harvest. Two or more lines in the same drop raise a combo and pay more. Cells score a little just for being planted.

The tray

When all three clusters have been placed, three new ones arrive. Leave a gap that a long cluster can still use. Corners fill fast if you only drop singles in the middle.
