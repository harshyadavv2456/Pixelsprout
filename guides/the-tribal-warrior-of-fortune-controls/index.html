The Tribal Warrior of Fortune Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

The Tribal Warrior of Fortune is an action-packed arcade game where players control a fierce warrior battling through ancient lands.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can use the mouse to aim and click to attack, while the keyboard's arrow keys or WASD keys are used for movement. The space bar might be used for special abilities or jumps.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game is played using touch controls, where tapping on the screen can be used to attack and swiping in different directions can control the warrior's movement.

Tips

  • Use rapid mouse clicks for quick attacks on desktop.
  • Swipe quickly on mobile for dodging enemy attacks.
  • Combine keyboard movement with mouse aiming on desktop for precise control.
