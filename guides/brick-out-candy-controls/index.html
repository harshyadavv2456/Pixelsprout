Brick Out Candy Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Brick Out Candy is a classic arcade-style game where players aim to clear levels by breaking bricks and collecting rewards.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can expect to use their mouse to aim and click to shoot, or possibly use keyboard keys such as space or enter to launch projectiles. The arrow keys or mouse movement may also be used to control the game's paddle or character.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, players will likely use touch controls to aim and shoot, with gestures such as tapping or swiping to control the game's actions.

Tips

  • Time your shots carefully to maximize brick destruction
  • Use the game's controls to precisely aim for specific bricks or power-ups
  • Keep an eye on the game's paddle or character movement to anticipate and react to incoming bricks or obstacles
