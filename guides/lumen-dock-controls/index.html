Lumen Dock - Controls

Advertisement

Touch

Tap the game to drop the sliding lantern. Tap the title screen to start, and tap again after a tip to retry. The canvas is built for a phone held upright.

Keyboard

Space or Enter drops. R starts a new run from the play screen. You do not steer the lantern. You only choose when it falls.

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button under the title to fill the screen. Press Esc to leave it.
