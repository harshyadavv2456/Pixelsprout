Alien Blocks - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Arcade

Alien Blocks is a fast-paced arcade game that challenges players to clear levels by strategically removing blocks, with the goal of achieving the highest score possible. As players progress, the blocks will fall faster and the grid will become increasingly complex.

  1. Focus on creating combinations of blocks that will clear multiple rows at once to maximize your score
  2. Try to keep the grid as flat as possible to prevent blocks from piling up and to create more opportunities for large combos
  3. Look for opportunities to create chain reactions by clearing blocks in a way that will cause other blocks to fall and clear
  4. Pay attention to the next few blocks that are going to be added to the grid and plan your moves accordingly
  5. Use any special blocks or power-ups strategically to help you get out of difficult situations or to clear a large number of blocks at once
  6. As the game speeds up, prioritize making quick and efficient moves to keep the grid under control
  7. Experiment with different strategies for handling different block patterns and shapes to find what works best for you

By mastering the art of clearing blocks efficiently and strategically, players can achieve high scores and unlock new levels and challenges in Alien Blocks.
