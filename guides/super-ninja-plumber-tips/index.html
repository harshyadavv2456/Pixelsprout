Super Ninja Plumber - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Arcade

In Super Ninja Plumber, a fast-paced arcade game, you control a ninja character with the unique twist of having plumbing skills, navigating through challenging levels filled with enemies and obstacles. Your goal is to clear each level by defeating enemies and fixing pipes to progress.

  1. Master the wall-jumping technique to navigate vertical sections of the levels efficiently, as it allows you to climb higher and reach hidden areas or secret pipes.
  2. Use your plumbing skills strategically to create new paths or distract enemies by fixing or breaking pipes at the right moment.
  3. Keep an eye on your ninja's stamina bar, as excessive use of special moves like the 'ninja dash' can leave you vulnerable to enemy attacks.
  4. Focus on taking out enemies in groups by using your surroundings to your advantage, such as by dropping objects on them or using the environment to funnel them into kill zones.
  5. Pay attention to the mini-map to anticipate upcoming obstacles and plan your approach accordingly, especially when dealing with moving platforms or traps.
  6. Collect power-ups like the 'wrench' to enhance your plumbing abilities or the 'shuriken' to increase your combat effectiveness.
  7. Practice precise timing for your jumps, as the game's tight platforming sections require accuracy to avoid taking unnecessary damage from spikes or pitfalls.

By combining your ninja skills with the creative use of plumbing abilities, you can overcome the challenges of Super Ninja Plumber and emerge victorious in this uniquely entertaining arcade game.
