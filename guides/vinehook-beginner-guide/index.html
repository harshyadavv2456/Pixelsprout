What you are doing

A paper lantern hangs from a vine and swings over a line of hooks. You do not steer the swing. You choose the moment it lets go, then hope the lantern drops onto the next hook.

A swing

The vine moves on its own, a little wider and a little faster as your score climbs. Watch the next hook, not the lantern glow. Release when the lantern is traveling toward that hook and still has height. It keeps the speed it had at the moment you let go, then gravity pulls it down.

Chains

Any catch scores. A catch close to the middle of the hook is a perfect and multiplies the next catch. An off-center catch still counts, but the chain goes back to one. The best scores come from a run of perfects, not from rushing.

When a run ends

If the lantern falls past the bottom of the screen, or flies off either side, the vine slips and the run is over. Your best score stays on this browser. Tap, or press Space, to start again from the first hook.
