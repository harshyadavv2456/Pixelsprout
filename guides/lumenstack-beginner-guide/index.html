What you are doing

Glowing lumen blocks slide left and right above a night tower. You time the drop so each new block lands on the top of the stack. The overlapping width becomes the new top. Perfect landings keep the full width and score extra.

Scoring

Every successful drop adds points based on how much of the block overlaps. Near-perfect alignment awards a perfect bonus that grows with consecutive perfects. The run ends when the overlap is too small and the stack falls.

Pace

Speed increases as your score rises. Early drops give you time to read the motion. Later you must anticipate the bounce at the edges and drop earlier.
