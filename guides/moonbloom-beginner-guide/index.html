What you are doing

You drop pieces into a six-column planter. Matching plants grow. The bed fills from the bottom. Clear the evening before it locks up.

A turn

The piece in your hand is shown above the bed. Choose a column. The piece falls until it lands. If three or more of the same plant touch by an edge, they grow into the next form and the score jumps. Gravity pulls anything above into the gap, which can start another chain.

Evenings

Each evening has a goal. Clear five of them. After that the monsoon keeps going and the score is what you chase. Rain can fall on a column and upgrade the top plant there. Pebbles sit in cells and only break when a neighboring merge cracks them, or when a moonbloom shatters the bed.

Forms

Seed, sprout, leafy, bud, bloom, then moonbloom. You only merge plants of the same form. A moonbloom is the top of the chain.
