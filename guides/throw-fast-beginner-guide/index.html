What is Throw Fast?

Arcade games like Throw Fast typically involve quick, simple actions repeated at an increasingly challenging pace, requiring players to time their moves perfectly to succeed. These games often feature simple yet addictive gameplay, making them easy to pick up but hard to master. Players must focus on reacting quickly to on-screen events.

First steps

  1. Start by familiarizing yourself with the game's controls and basic mechanics.
  2. Practice throwing at the right moment to hit your targets accurately.
