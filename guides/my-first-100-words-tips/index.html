My First 100 Words - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Educational

Getting started with My First 100 Words? Here's what to focus on early.

  1. Learn the core controls before chasing a high score in My First 100 Words.
  2. Take it slow on your first few runs to understand the game's rhythm.
  3. Watch for patterns - most challenges repeat in a predictable way.
  4. Don't rush upgrades or purchases until you understand what matters most.
  5. Replay early levels once you've learned the mechanics - scores improve fast.

With a bit of practice, My First 100 Words becomes much easier to pick up.
