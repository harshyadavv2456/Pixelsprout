Dogs vs Aliens Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Addictive

Here's how to control Dogs vs Aliens on both desktop and mobile.

Desktop controls

Use your mouse and keyboard as prompted on-screen when the game loads.

Mobile controls

Tap and swipe directly on the game screen - most controls map to simple touch gestures.

Tips

  • Fullscreen mode usually makes touch controls more accurate.
  • If controls feel unresponsive, try reloading the page once.
