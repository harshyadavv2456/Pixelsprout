Escape The Sewer Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

In Escape The Sewer, players navigate through a challenging underground environment to reach the surface.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can use the keyboard to move and jump, with possible additional keys for actions like crouching or interacting with objects, while the mouse may be used for aiming or selecting items.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, touch controls likely involve tapping or swiping to move, jump, or perform actions, with the screen divided into areas for different commands.

Tips

  • Time your jumps carefully to avoid obstacles and pitfalls.
  • Use the available controls to explore your surroundings and find hidden paths or items.
  • Mastering the movement controls is key to progressing through the sewer system.
