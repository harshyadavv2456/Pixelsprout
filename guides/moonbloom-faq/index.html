What is Moonbloom?

A Pixelsprout original puzzle. You drop seeds into a night planter and grow them by matching three or more.

How do I score?

A merge scores from the plant form and the size of the group. A chain, where one merge makes another, multiplies that score.

What is a pebble?

A pebble blocks one cell. It does not merge. A merge next to it cracks it. A moonbloom breaks pebbles across the bed.

What does rain do?

Rain upgrades the highest plant in the column it hits, unless that plant is already a moonbloom.

Is there a win?

Clear five evenings. After that you can keep playing for a higher score until the bed fills.

Does it save?

Your best score stays in this browser. Clearing the site data clears the score.

Is it free, and does it work on a phone?

Yes. Nothing to install. Tap a column to play. It also works with a keyboard.
