Xmas Furious - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Arcade

In Xmas Furious, a fast-paced arcade game, you must navigate a chaotic winter wonderland filled with obstacles and challenges to rescue Santa's stolen gifts. Your reflexes and timing will be put to the test as you dash through snow-covered levels.

  1. Master the art of jumping and sliding to avoid icy patches and pits that can slow you down or instantly kill your character.
  2. Keep an eye on the mini-map to anticipate upcoming obstacles and plan your route accordingly, especially when approaching narrow tunnels or crowded areas.
  3. Use power-ups like the 'Snow Shield' to protect yourself from harm or the 'Gift Grab' to attract nearby presents, but be mindful of their limited duration and strategic use.
  4. Learn the patterns of enemy characters like the 'Naughty Elf' who throws snowballs or the 'Reindeer' that charges at you, to time your jumps and dodges effectively.
  5. Maintain your speed by avoiding collisions with objects and other characters, as losing momentum can make it harder to reach the next checkpoint or complete a level within the time limit.
  6. In levels with multiple paths, choose the one that leads to the most gifts or power-ups to maximize your score and progress.
  7. Practice handling your character's movements, especially when turning or changing direction quickly, to improve overall control and responsiveness.

By following these strategies and honing your skills, you'll be well on your way to becoming a Xmas Furious champion, rescuing all of Santa's gifts and saving Christmas.
