Webskii Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Webskii is a fast-paced arcade game where players navigate through challenging levels using quick reflexes and precise control.

Desktop controls

On desktop, Webskii can be controlled using the mouse for navigation and the keyboard for actions like jumping or shooting, allowing for precise and swift movements. The W, A, S, D keys or the arrow keys may also be used for character movement.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, Webskii utilizes touch controls, where players can tap, swipe, or hold on the screen to control their character, with some actions potentially requiring multi-touch gestures.

Tips

  • Use short, precise mouse movements for better control on desktop.
  • On mobile, try to keep your finger close to the action to minimize response time.
  • Practice switching between different control inputs smoothly to master the game.
