Ladder Race 3D Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Ladder Race 3D is a fast-paced arcade game where players compete to reach the top of a challenging course.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can use the keyboard to control their character's movements, such as using the arrow keys or WASD keys to move left and right, and the space bar to jump or perform actions.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game can be controlled by tapping or swiping on the screen to move the character, with gestures potentially used for jumping or other actions.

Tips

  • Time your jumps carefully to avoid obstacles and maintain speed.
  • Use precise movements to navigate through tight spaces and narrow platforms.
  • Adjust your movement speed according to the situation to achieve the best results.
