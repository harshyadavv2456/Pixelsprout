Ladder Race Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Ladder Race is a fast-paced arcade game where players navigate through increasingly challenging levels by climbing ladders and avoiding obstacles.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can use the mouse to click on ladders to climb or the keyboard's arrow keys to move left and right, with spacebar potentially used for jumping or other special actions.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game utilizes touch controls, where tapping on the left or right side of the screen moves the character in the corresponding direction, and tapping on specific areas might trigger actions like jumping.

Tips

  • Click or tap precisely to land on small ladders.
  • Use keyboard controls for more precise movements on desktop.
  • Master the timing of your jumps to overcome obstacles efficiently.
