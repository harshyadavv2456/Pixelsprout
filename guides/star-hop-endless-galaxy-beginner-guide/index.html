Star Hop: Endless Galaxy Beginner's Guide - How to Get Started

New to Star Hop: Endless Galaxy? Here is what is on the first screen, how the guided flight teaches you, and what the How to play screen adds.

What is Star Hop: Endless Galaxy?

Star Hop: Endless Galaxy is a free one-button space arcade game that runs on the creator's own site and is embedded on Pixelsprout. Planets sit in a column going up the screen. Your ship circles the current planet with a line pointing ahead, and a well-timed click sends it along that line to the next planet. A green line means you will reach it, and a gold line means a PERFECT hop that earns extra points and builds a combo. The flight keeps going upward with new planets.

First steps

  1. On the title screen, find the ENDLESS FLIGHT card at the bottom. It says Start here, your first flight is guided.
  2. Press PLAY on that card.
  3. Follow the prompts. Click when the game says Now! for the first hop.
  4. When you see Your turn, click on your own as soon as the line turns green.
  5. If you see Missed - try again, wait for the line to come around and click again.
  6. Open the three-line menu and choose How to play to read the full rules.

Who it's for

Players who like short timing games with one control. The guided first flight makes it easy to start, and the combo system rewards a steady hand.

The title screen

A new player sees a Pilot badge with level 1 and a progress bar in the top left, a stardust counter reading 0 in the top right, and the three-line menu button beside it. The ENDLESS FLIGHT card with its PLAY button sits at the bottom.

What the How to play screen lists

The help screen describes a Hangar where stardust is spent on upgrades and ships, a Daily challenge with one galaxy for everyone each day, Missions with three tasks for rewards, and Levels that each give a crate and raise your score multiplier up to x2. These were read on the help screen and were not tried during the first flight.
