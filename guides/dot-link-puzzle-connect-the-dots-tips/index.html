Dot Link Puzzle - Connect the Dots Tips and Strategy

Dot Link Puzzle - Connect the DotsPuzzle · Free to play

Dot Link Puzzle - Connect the Dots asks you to join each pair of same-colored squares with a line and cover every cell of the grid. These tips come from playing the first Beginner boards, a second 5 by 5 board with a hint, and a look at the first Blockers board.

  1. Draw the short, obvious lines first. On Level 1 the green pair sat two cells apart in the left column and the red pair filled the whole bottom row, so those lines were fixed before any thinking was needed.
  2. Use the long, bendy line to soak up the empty cells. On Level 1 the yellow line ran left along the top row, down the second column and back right to its partner, which used up the cells nothing else could reach.
  3. Watch the Fill counter, not just the Lines counter. A board is only solved when every cell is covered, so a level can have all pairs joined and still not be done if Fill is below 100%.
  4. Do not drag through another color's line unless you mean to. When a line was pulled across the cyan and red lines, both of them were cut short and had to be drawn again.
  5. Press UNDO right after a bad drag. In testing it put the cut lines back the way they were, which is much faster than redrawing several colors.
  6. Save the HINT button for boards that stump you. It starts with 5 uses, one press drew a complete line for one color, and the count stayed at 4 when moving to a different bundle.
  7. Count the cells before you commit to a route. On a 5 by 5 board there are 25 cells, and every one has to belong to some color by the end.

Lock in the forced lines, route the longest line through the leftover space, and use UNDO the moment a drag goes wrong. The Fill counter tells you when the board is really finished.

How Level 1 was solved

Level 1 in the Beginner bundle is a 5 by 5 board with green, yellow, orange, cyan and red pairs. Green joins straight down the left column, cyan runs across the fourth row, and red runs along the whole bottom row.

That leaves orange and yellow. Orange goes right from its square near the center, then down the right edge to its partner. Yellow starts in the top right corner, runs left along the top row, drops down the second column and turns right to reach its partner. With those five lines Fill reads 100% and the LEVEL COMPLETE popup appears.

What the hint does

On Level 2 the HINT button drew the full yellow line between its two squares and marked both ends with a blue star. The Moves counter went up by one and the red badge on the button dropped from 5 to 4.
