Somersault Ninja-Samurai Ninja Jump - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Arcade

In Somersault Ninja-Samurai Ninja Jump, a fast-paced arcade game, you take on the role of a ninja-samurai hybrid, navigating through challenging levels filled with obstacles and enemies, requiring precise timing and jumps to succeed. Your goal is to jump, flip, and slash your way through increasingly difficult terrain.

  1. Master the art of somersaulting to maintain momentum and avoid taking damage from enemies or spikes, as this move allows you to jump higher and cover more distance.
  2. Use your ninja stars wisely, as they can be used to defeat enemies from a distance, but also keep an eye on your ammo to avoid running out during critical moments.
  3. Pay attention to the level design, looking for patterns in the placement of enemies and obstacles to anticipate and plan your jumps and attacks in advance.
  4. Time your jumps carefully to avoid colliding with moving platforms or enemies, and use the environment to your advantage by bouncing off walls or using slopes to gain speed.
  5. Learn to recognize and counter different enemy types, such as the flying enemies that require a well-timed slash or the ground enemies that can be taken out with a somersault attack.
  6. Keep an eye on your health bar and seek out health power-ups or safe zones when your health is low, as the game can become very challenging if you're not at full health.
  7. Practice your slashing technique to maximize your score by taking out multiple enemies at once and to clear a path through densely packed levels.

By following these tips and practicing your skills, you'll be well on your way to becoming a master ninja-samurai in Somersault Ninja-Samurai Ninja Jump, conquering even the most daunting challenges the game has to offer.
