New to Flappy Bird 3D? This covers what you need to know before your first playthrough.

What is Flappy Bird 3D?

Flappy Bird 3D is a animal game you can play instantly in your browser, no download required.

First steps

  1. Start the game and get familiar with the main screen.
  2. Play through the first level or round without worrying about your score.
  3. Check the in-game instructions if you're unsure what to do next.

Who it's for

Fans of animal games looking for something quick to play.
