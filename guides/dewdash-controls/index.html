Dewdash - Controls

Keyboard

Left Arrow or A moves one lane left. Right Arrow or D moves one lane right. Up Arrow, W, Space, or Enter hops. Down Arrow or S ducks. Enter or Space also starts and restarts a run.

Touch

Swipe sideways to change lanes. Swipe down to duck a branch. Tap, or swipe up, to hop a root and to start or restart. A short tap with almost no movement is a hop.

Fullscreen

The Fullscreen button on the game page expands the play frame. The same keyboard and touch controls work there.
