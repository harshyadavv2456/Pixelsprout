Snake - Frequently Asked Questions

Guide · Arcade

Is Snake free to play?

Yes, Snake is a free-to-play game that can be played online without any subscription or purchase requirements. You can start playing immediately in your web browser.

Can I play Snake on my mobile device?

Yes, Snake can be played on mobile devices, including smartphones and tablets, as long as they have a compatible web browser. The game's simple controls make it easy to play on touch screens.

How do I control the snake in the game?

You can control the snake using the arrow keys or by swiping in the direction you want the snake to move on a touch screen. The snake will continue to move in the direction of the last input.

Does the game get progressively harder?

Yes, the game becomes increasingly difficult as the snake grows longer and the playing field becomes more crowded with obstacles. Your reaction time and strategic thinking will be tested as the game speeds up.

What makes Snake so fun to play?

The game's simplicity, combined with its challenging and addictive nature, makes it enjoyable to play. The satisfaction of beating your previous high score and competing with others for the top spot adds to the fun.

Why does my snake sometimes run into itself?

This happens when you accidentally turn the snake back into itself, causing a collision. To avoid this, make sure to plan your turns ahead of time and be careful not to make sharp turns when the snake is moving quickly.

How do I score points in the game?

You score points by eating the food pellets that appear on the screen, which causes your snake to grow longer. The longer your snake, the more points you will earn for each pellet you eat.
