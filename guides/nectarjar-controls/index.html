Nectarjar - Controls

Touch

Drag or slide along the jar to move the orb on the rim. Tap to drop it. The first tap on the title screen starts a run. Hold the phone upright.

Mouse

Move the pointer to aim. Click to drop. Click the title or the spilled screen to start again.

Keyboard

Left and Right move the aim. Space or Enter drops. On the title screen, Space or Enter starts. R starts a fresh jar at any time.

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button under the title to fill the screen. Press Esc to leave it.
