DOP Rainbow Friends - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Kids

Getting started with DOP Rainbow Friends? Here's what to focus on early.

  1. Learn the core controls before chasing a high score in DOP Rainbow Friends.
  2. Take it slow on your first few runs to understand the game's rhythm.
  3. Watch for patterns - most challenges repeat in a predictable way.
  4. Don't rush upgrades or purchases until you understand what matters most.
  5. Replay early levels once you've learned the mechanics - scores improve fast.

With a bit of practice, DOP Rainbow Friends becomes much easier to pick up.
