What you are doing

A lantern hangs from a reed over a night pond. It swings on its own. You choose the moment it lets go, and it flies toward the next reed.

A swing

The lantern moves in an arc. Release near the forward side of the swing so the arc carries it onto the next hook. Release too early and it drops short. Release too late and it sails past.

Perfect catches

A catch close to the center of the reed is a perfect. Perfects stack into a chain and add extra points. A ordinary catch still scores, but the chain resets.

The run

Each catch moves you along the pond and the swing speeds up a little. Miss the reed, or leave the pond, and the run ends. Tap to start another. The best score stays in this browser.
