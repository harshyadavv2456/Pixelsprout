Idle Military Base. Army Tycoon Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Strategy

In Idle Military Base. Army Tycoon, players manage and grow their military base through strategic decisions and efficient resource allocation.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can use their mouse to navigate menus, select buildings, and manage resources, while keyboard shortcuts may be available for common actions like saving progress or accessing settings.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, touch controls allow players to tap on buildings to select them, swipe to navigate the base, and pinch to zoom in and out for a better view.

Tips

  • Regularly check and manage your resource production to maintain a steady income
  • Use your mouse or touch controls to carefully plan the layout of your base for optimal efficiency
  • Keep an eye on your base's progress and adjust your strategy as needed to maximize growth
