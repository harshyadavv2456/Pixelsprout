Cinderwell - Controls

CinderwellArcade · Free to play
Play now
Advertisement

Touch

Tap anywhere on the game to drop the swinging coal. On the title screen, tap to start. On the game-over screen, tap to play again. The game is built for a phone held upright.

Mouse

Click the game to drop. Click the title screen to start, and click the game-over screen to restart.

Keyboard

Space or Enter drops the coal, starts a run, and restarts after the fire goes out. Arrow keys are not used. The vine moves on its own.

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button under the title to fill the screen. Press Esc to leave it.
