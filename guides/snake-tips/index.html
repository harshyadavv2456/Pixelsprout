Snake - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Arcade

In the classic arcade game Snake, players control a growing snake that must navigate a grid to consume food pellets while avoiding collisions with the wall or itself. As the snake grows, the challenge increases, requiring strategic thinking and quick reflexes to succeed.

  1. Start by focusing on consuming the food pellets in a way that allows your snake to grow in a controlled manner, avoiding sharp turns and narrow passages.
  2. Use the grid lines to your advantage by navigating your snake along the edges of the screen to reduce the risk of collision.
  3. Plan your route in advance, taking into account the location of the next food pellet and any obstacles in your path.
  4. As your snake grows, prioritize avoiding collisions with your own tail, which can be just as deadly as the wall.
  5. Make use of the empty spaces on the grid to create temporary safe zones, giving you time to plan your next move.
  6. Be prepared to make sharp turns when necessary, but try to maintain a consistent direction to minimize the risk of collision.
  7. Keep an eye on the available space on the grid, adjusting your strategy as the game progresses and the snake grows longer.

By mastering these strategies and adapting to the changing game environment, players can extend their survival time and achieve high scores in the addictive and challenging game of Snake.
