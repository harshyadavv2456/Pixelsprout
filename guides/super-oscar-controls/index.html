Super Oscar Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Super Oscar is a fast-paced arcade game where players control the character Oscar through increasingly challenging levels.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can expect to use the mouse to navigate menus and the keyboard to control Oscar's movements, such as using the arrow keys to move and the space bar to jump.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, touch controls allow players to tap on the screen to jump and swipe left or right to move Oscar, with the game potentially including on-screen buttons for other actions.

Tips

  • Use precise timing with your jumps to avoid obstacles.
  • Mastering the dash move can help you overcome difficult sections.
  • Keep an eye on your character's position relative to the screen edges to avoid accidental moves off-screen.
