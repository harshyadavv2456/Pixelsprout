Dewlane - Controls

Advertisement

Touch

Tap the left side of the canvas to hop one lane left. Tap the right side to hop one lane right. A tap in the middle does not change lanes during a run. On the title screen or after a miss, any tap starts a run.

Keyboard

Left arrow or A hops left. Right arrow or D hops right. Space or Enter starts a run from the title screen and restarts after the lane closes. The game does not use a jump key. Lane position is the only move.

Fullscreen

The Fullscreen button on the game page expands the canvas. Controls stay the same in fullscreen.
