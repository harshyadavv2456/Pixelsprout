Fly and Shoot 1. Italian Bosses Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Fly and Shoot 1: Italian Bosses is an action-packed arcade game where you control a vehicle to destroy enemies and overcome challenges.

Desktop controls

On desktop, you can likely control your vehicle using the mouse for direction and keyboard for shooting or other actions, with possible additional keys for special abilities or boosting.

Mobile controls

On mobile, the game probably utilizes touch controls, where you tap, hold, or swipe on the screen to control your vehicle's movement and actions.

Tips

  • Use precise mouse movements on desktop or finger swipes on mobile to navigate through tight spaces and avoid enemy fire.
  • Time your shots well to take out enemies efficiently, especially when facing tougher foes like bosses.
  • Keep an eye on your vehicle's position and the screen's edges to avoid accidental collisions or getting stuck.
