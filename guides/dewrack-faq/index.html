What is Dewrack?

A Pixelsprout original arcade game. You catch falling dew on a leaf rack and fuse matching drops.

How do I score?

A catch is worth a little. A fuse scores from the new form, and a chain multiplies that score while the combo holds.

What are the drop forms?

Bead, pearl, orb, lantern, moon, and tide. Only two of the same form fuse. Tide is the largest.

Why did I lose a life?

A drop missed the rack, or a settled drop rolled off an end. Three of those end the run.

Is there a win?

There is no last level. The drops come a little faster as the score climbs. The run is about the best score.

Does it save?

Your best score stays in this browser under the key dewrack-best. Clearing site data clears it.

Is it free, and does it work on a phone?

Yes. Nothing to install. Drag to move the rack. It also works with a keyboard.

Can I play it fullscreen?

Yes. The Fullscreen button under the title fills the screen. Esc leaves fullscreen.
