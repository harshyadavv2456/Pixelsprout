The dead among us Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

The dead among us is a fast-paced arcade game that challenges players to survive in a thrilling environment.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players will likely use the mouse to aim and interact with the game world, while the keyboard is used for movement and other actions. Common keyboard controls might include the arrow keys or WASD keys for movement.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, players will use touch controls to navigate and interact with the game, with gestures such as tapping, swiping, or holding to perform different actions.

Tips

  • Use quick and precise mouse movements to accurately target and interact with objects in the game world.
  • Familiarize yourself with the keyboard layout to efficiently move and perform actions.
  • Adjust your touch sensitivity on mobile to suit your playing style for better control.
