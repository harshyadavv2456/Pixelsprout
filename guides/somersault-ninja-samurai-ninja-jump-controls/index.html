Somersault Ninja-Samurai Ninja Jump Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Somersault Ninja-Samurai Ninja Jump is a fast-paced arcade game where players control a ninja character as it jumps and somersaults through challenging levels.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players will likely use the space bar or mouse clicks to jump and perform somersaults, with potential keyboard controls for left and right movement. Additional keyboard keys could be used for special abilities or power-ups.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game will utilize touch controls, where players tap the screen to jump and swipe gestures to control the ninja's movement and perform somersaults.

Tips

  • Timing your jumps and somersaults is crucial to avoid obstacles and reach the furthest distance.
  • Mastering the somersault move can help you gain extra speed and height.
  • Keep an eye on your character's momentum to adjust your controls accordingly.
