Ghost Runaway Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Ghost Runaway is a fast-paced arcade game that challenges players to navigate through increasingly difficult levels.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players will likely use the keyboard to control movement and jumps, with possible additional keys for actions like pausing the game or interacting with objects.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game will likely utilize touch controls, such as tapping or swiping to move, jump, or perform in-game actions.

Tips

  • Use precise timing to navigate obstacles and avoid taking damage.
  • Mastering the jump mechanics will be crucial to progressing through the game.
  • Familiarize yourself with the game's controls to improve your reaction time and overall performance.
