Prison Escape Plan Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

In Prison Escape Plan, you must navigate a challenging environment to escape from prison, using your wits and quick reflexes to overcome obstacles and avoid detection.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can use the mouse to navigate and interact with the environment, while the keyboard can be used for actions such as running or hiding. The specific keys used may vary, but common controls include the arrow keys or WASD keys for movement.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, touch controls allow players to tap or swipe to navigate and interact with the environment, with gestures potentially used for actions such as jumping or dodging.

Tips

  • Use the environment to your advantage by hiding in shadows or behind objects to avoid detection.
  • Time your movements carefully to avoid being seen by guards or caught in traps.
  • Experiment with different actions, such as running or sneaking, to find the best approach for each situation.
