Petalstack FAQ

Is Petalstack free?

Yes. It is a free Pixelsprout original that runs in the browser with no download or account required.

How do I score points?

Each successful stack gives points. A perfect alignment (very close to the center) gives a higher bonus and keeps the full petal width.

What happens when I miss?

If the dropped petal has almost no overlap with the one below, the tower falls and the run ends. You can restart immediately.

Does the game get harder?

Yes. The moving petal speeds up as the tower grows taller.

Is my high score saved?

Yes. The best score is stored in your browser's local storage and shown on the title and game screens.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Tap the screen to drop petals. It works on phones, tablets, and desktop.

Is there a time limit?

No. The game is endless. Your goal is the highest score before the tower falls.
