Keyboard

Left and Right, or A and D, move the drop column. Enter, Space, or Down drops the stone. R starts a new run from the title or after the basin fills.

Touch and mouse

Tap or click a column to drop there. On the title screen and the game-over screen, a tap or Enter starts the next run.

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button on the game page. The basin scales to the frame. Touch still maps across the six columns.

Score

The current score and your best sit under the title. Best is saved in this browser only. Clearing site data clears it.
