Jewel Legend Beginner's Guide - How to Get Started

Guide · Bejeweled

New to Jewel Legend? This covers what you need to know before your first playthrough.

What is Jewel Legend?

Jewel Legend is a bejeweled game you can play instantly in your browser, no download required.

First steps

  1. Start the game and get familiar with the main screen.
  2. Play through the first level or round without worrying about your score.
  3. Check the in-game instructions if you're unsure what to do next.

Who it's for

Fans of bejeweled games looking for something quick to play.
