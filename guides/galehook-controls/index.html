Touch

Tap anywhere on the canvas to release the vine. On the title screen and the game-over screen, the same tap starts a new run. The page uses touch-action none so a tap does not scroll the game.

Keyboard

Space, Enter, or Arrow Up releases the vine and also restarts from the title or the game-over screen. There is no left or right control. Timing is the whole move.

Fullscreen

The Fullscreen button on the game bar expands the canvas. Release still works with a tap or Space inside that frame.
