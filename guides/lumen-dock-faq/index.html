What is Lumen Dock?

A Pixelsprout original arcade game. You drop paper lanterns onto a night dock and keep the stack from tipping.

How do I score?

Each drop scores from the width that stayed, times a steady combo. A near-perfect landing raises the combo.

What is a steady?

A steady is a drop that lands almost flush with the lantern below. It keeps the full width instead of trimming the overhang.

Why did the run end?

The sliding lantern missed the one below, or only a sliver overlapped. The stack tips and the score is locked.

Does it get faster?

Yes. The slide speeds up a little after every successful drop, up to a cap.

Does it save?

Your best score stays in this browser under local storage. Clearing site data clears the score.

Is it free, and does it work on a phone?

Yes. Nothing to install. Tap to drop. It also works with a keyboard.

Is this a copy of another stack game?

No. Lumen Dock is an original Pixelsprout night-dock lantern stack. It uses a timing drop, not a branded character or a copied level set.
