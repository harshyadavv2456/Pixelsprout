Save the Galaxy Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Save the Galaxy is an action-packed arcade game where players navigate through challenging levels to rescue the galaxy from various threats.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can expect to use the mouse to aim and shoot, and the keyboard to control movement and potentially access special abilities or power-ups.

Mobile controls

On mobile, touch controls will likely involve tapping to shoot and dragging to move the character or spaceship, with possibly additional gestures for special actions.

Tips

  • Use precise mouse movements to accurately target enemies on desktop.
  • On mobile, try to keep your finger closer to the movement area to avoid accidental shots.
  • Mastering the timing of your shots and movements is key to overcoming obstacles and defeating enemies.
