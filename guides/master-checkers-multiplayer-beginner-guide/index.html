Master Checkers Multiplayer Beginner's Guide - How to Get Started

Guide · Checkers

New to Master Checkers Multiplayer? This covers what you need to know before your first playthrough.

What is Master Checkers Multiplayer?

Master Checkers Multiplayer is a checkers game you can play instantly in your browser, no download required.

First steps

  1. Start the game and get familiar with the main screen.
  2. Play through the first level or round without worrying about your score.
  3. Check the in-game instructions if you're unsure what to do next.

Who it's for

Fans of checkers games looking for something quick to play.
