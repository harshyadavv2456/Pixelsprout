Brick Blaze Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Brick Blaze is a fast-paced arcade game where players destroy bricks by bouncing a ball with a paddle.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can use the mouse to move the paddle left and right, and the keyboard to pause or resume the game.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, players can use touch controls to move the paddle by swiping their finger across the screen, with optional on-screen buttons for other actions.

Tips

  • Use the paddle's movement speed to your advantage by timing your moves carefully
  • Aim for the bricks at the edges to get the most out of each bounce
  • Be prepared to move quickly to catch the ball when it bounces off at sharp angles
