City Building Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Strategy

City Building is a strategy game where players design, manage, and grow their own city from scratch, making key decisions to balance resources and population growth.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can expect to use their mouse to navigate menus, select buildings, and manage city layout, with keyboard shortcuts potentially available for common actions like zooming or switching between different city views.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game likely utilizes touch controls, allowing players to tap to select buildings, pinch to zoom, and swipe to navigate through the city or access different menus and options.

Tips

  • Use the mouse or touch controls to carefully plan and manage your city's layout to optimize resource allocation and population density.
  • Regularly check and manage your resource levels to avoid shortages that could hinder city growth.
  • Take advantage of any available shortcuts or menus to streamline your gameplay and focus on strategic decision-making.
