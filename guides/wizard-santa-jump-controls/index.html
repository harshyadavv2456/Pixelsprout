Wizard Santa Jump Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Wizard Santa Jump is a fun arcade game where you control a character as it jumps and navigates through obstacles.

Desktop controls

On desktop, you can likely control your character using the mouse to tap or click to jump, or use the keyboard's space bar or arrow keys to navigate and jump.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game probably uses touch controls, such as tapping on the screen to jump or swiping left and right to move your character.

Tips

  • Time your jumps carefully to avoid hitting obstacles
  • Use the right amount of movement to stay on platforms and avoid falling
  • Practice your timing and navigation to improve your overall score
