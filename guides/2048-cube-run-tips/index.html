2048 Cube Run - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

2048 Cube Run2048 · Free to play
Play now
Advertisement

2048 Cube Run mixes two ideas: a runner that moves forward on its own and the number-merging goal of 2048. These tips come from clearing the first six levels in one sitting, and they stick to what showed up on screen. Runs were short, so the best help is knowing what each screen is telling you.

  1. Click Ok, Play Now! and wait for the loading counter to finish before you do anything else. The game only appeared after the counter reached 100%, and clicking around early did nothing useful.
  2. Press CLICK TO START! to begin a run. After that the cube runs forward by itself, so you do not need to hold a key to keep moving.
  3. Read the numbers on the cubes as you approach them. Values I saw ran from 2, 4, 8 and 16 on early levels up to 512 and 4096 later, and the page says the goal is to merge identical numbers.
  4. Look ahead at the track layout. Later levels put more cubes in the side lanes and used larger values, so a quick glance at what is coming tells you how busy the run will be.
  5. Watch for the checkered finish line. Past it, colored strips labeled x2, x3 and x4 appear, and a bonus wheel at the end showed x2, x3, x4, x3 and x2.
  6. Check the coin total at the start of each level to see your progress. Mine read 500, 650, 770, 840, 920 and 990 on levels one to six.
  7. Treat WATCH ADS & GET as optional. The result screen also has a NEXT LEVEL button, and that moved me on to the next level.

Steering was the one thing I could not confirm, so keep your expectations modest and watch what your cube does on each run. If you find a method that changes lanes reliably, it is worth noting for later levels.

What the numbers on the cubes are telling you

Every cube carries a number, and the in-game text says to merge identical numbered cubes on the way to 2048. On the first levels the numbers were small, such as 2, 4, 8 and 16 stacked in a triangle shape ahead of the player.

By level six the side lanes held bigger stacks, and I also saw a 4096 cube. Because I could not steer reliably, I cannot say how merging plays out when you pick your own lane, so treat the numbers as a preview of what is coming rather than a plan.
