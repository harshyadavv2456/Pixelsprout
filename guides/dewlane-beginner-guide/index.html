What you are doing

You are a sprout on a night garden path with three dew lanes. The path scrolls toward you. Your job is to stay in an open lane and pick up dew.

A hop

Tap the left half of the game to hop left. Tap the right half to hop right. Arrow keys and A and D do the same. You can only be in one of the three lanes.

Roots and dew

Brown root bars close a lane. If a bar passes through your sprout, the run ends. Pale drops are worth extra score. Travel itself also adds score, so a long clean run beats a short greedy one.

The climb

The garden speeds up the farther you go. Late hops get caught by roots that used to be easy. Your best score stays in this browser.
