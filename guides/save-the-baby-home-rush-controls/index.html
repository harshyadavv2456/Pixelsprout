Save the Baby Home Rush Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Save the Baby Home Rush is a fast-paced arcade game where you must navigate through challenging levels to reach your goal.

Desktop controls

On desktop, you can likely use the mouse to click on objects or navigate through the environment, and the keyboard to perform actions such as jumping or moving your character.

Mobile controls

On mobile, you can use touch controls to tap on the screen to interact with objects, swipe to move your character, or use virtual buttons to perform actions.

Tips

  • Time your movements carefully to avoid obstacles and enemies
  • Use your controls to adjust your speed and positioning
  • Pay attention to the game environment to anticipate and react to upcoming challenges
