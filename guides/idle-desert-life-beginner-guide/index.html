New to Idle Desert Life? This covers what you need to know before your first playthrough.

What is Idle Desert Life?

Idle Desert Life is a building game you can play instantly in your browser, no download required.

First steps

  1. Start the game and get familiar with the main screen.
  2. Play through the first level or round without worrying about your score.
  3. Check the in-game instructions if you're unsure what to do next.

Who it's for

Fans of building games looking for something quick to play.
