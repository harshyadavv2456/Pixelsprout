Noob vs Hacker Zombie Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Noob vs Hacker Zombie is a fast-paced arcade game where players must navigate through challenging levels filled with zombies.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players can expect to use the mouse to aim and shoot, and the keyboard to move their character and perform other actions. The W, A, S, and D keys or the arrow keys are likely used for movement.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, the game is controlled using touch gestures, such as tapping to shoot or swipe to move the character.

Tips

  • Use quick and precise mouse movements to aim at zombies on desktop.
  • Time your taps or clicks to shoot zombies efficiently, taking into account any reload times.
  • Adjust your movement speed according to the situation to avoid obstacles and zombies.
