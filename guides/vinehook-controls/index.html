Vinehook - Controls

Advertisement

Touch

Tap anywhere on the game to start, to let the lantern go, and to restart after a fall. You do not drag the vine. One tap is one release.

Keyboard

Press Space or Enter to start, release the lantern, and restart. Arrow keys are not used. The swing is automatic.

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button under the title to fill the screen. Press Esc to leave it.
