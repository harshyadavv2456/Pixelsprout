Guilty Sniper Controls - How to Play (Desktop & Mobile)

Guide · Arcade

Guilty Sniper is a fast-paced arcade game where players take on the role of a sniper, aiming to complete missions by taking out targets with precision and speed.

Desktop controls

On desktop, players will likely use their mouse to aim and shoot, with keyboard controls for actions like reloading or switching between scopes. The W, A, S, D keys or arrow keys might also be used for character movement or camera adjustments.

Mobile controls

On mobile devices, players will use touch controls, tapping on the screen to shoot and possibly swiping to adjust the scope or move the character. The game may also support pinch-to-zoom for better aiming.

Tips

  • Use the right mouse button or a designated key for zooming in to get a clearer shot on desktop.
  • On mobile, try tapping and holding to steady your aim before releasing to shoot.
  • Keep an eye on your ammo and reload promptly to avoid being caught off guard by incoming enemies.
