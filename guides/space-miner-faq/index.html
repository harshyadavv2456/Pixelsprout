Space Miner is likely available on both desktop and mobile platforms, including smartphones and tablets, allowing players to access the game from various devices. Check the game's official website or app stores for specific mobile support.

The controls for Space Miner typically involve using keyboard or touchscreen inputs to navigate and interact with the game environment, such as moving the spaceship, shooting, and collecting resources. The exact controls may vary depending on the device and platform being used.

Space Miner, as an arcade game, is designed to be challenging but accessible, with increasing difficulty as players progress through levels or achieve higher scores. The game's difficulty curve is intended to keep players engaged and motivated to improve their skills.
