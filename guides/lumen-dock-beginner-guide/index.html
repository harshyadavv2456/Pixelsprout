What you are doing

A lantern slides left and right above the dock. You drop it onto the lantern beneath. The part that does not overlap is trimmed away. Stack as high as you can before the next drop misses.

A drop

Wait until the sliding lantern lines up with the one below, then tap or press Space. If less than a sliver overlaps, the stack tips and the run ends. A wider overlap leaves a wider lantern for the next drop.

Steadies

Land almost flush and the drop is a steady. A steady does not trim the lantern, adds a combo, and scores more. The slide gets faster after every successful drop, so early steadies matter.

The dock

The view follows the stack up the river. The base lantern stays wide. Later lanterns are only as wide as your last good overlap, unless you just landed a steady.
