Is Lumenstack free?

Yes. It is a free Pixelsprout original that runs in your browser with no download or account required.

How do I get a perfect drop?

Drop when the moving block is almost exactly aligned with the top of the tower. A small margin still counts as perfect and keeps the full width.

Why does the stack fall?

If the overlap between the dropped block and the top is less than about 12 pixels, the stack is considered unstable and the run ends.

Does my best score save?

Yes. Your highest score is stored in this browser's local storage and shown on the title and game-over screens.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Tap the screen to drop. The game is designed for both touch and keyboard.

Is there an end?

No. The game is endless. The speed keeps rising and the challenge is to push your best score higher.
