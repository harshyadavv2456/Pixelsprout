What is Petalrack?

Petalrack is a Pixelsprout original puzzle. You place blossom clusters on a night rack and harvest full rows and columns.

Do I need to download it?

No. It runs in the browser on this page. Nothing is installed.

How does scoring work?

Each planted cell adds a few points. A harvested line adds more, and extra lines in the same drop multiply that harvest.

Why did the rack end?

None of the clusters still in the tray fit on empty soil. A new rack starts from the title overlay.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in this browser, under the name petalrack-best. Clearing site data clears it.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. Tap the tray, then tap the bed. The canvas uses touch and does not scroll while you play.

Is this a copy of another block game?

No. Petalrack is an original garden rack: blossom clusters, harvest lines, and a three-piece tray. It does not use another game's name, characters, or art.
