Petalstack Controls

PetalstackArcade · Free to play
Play now
Advertisement

Petalstack uses simple tap or keyboard controls. No mouse movement is required.

Touch / Mouse

  • Tap or click anywhere on the game canvas to drop the current petal.
  • On the title or game-over screen, the same tap starts or restarts the game.

Keyboard

  • Space or Enter drops the petal (or starts / restarts).

Fullscreen

Use the Fullscreen button on the game page for a larger view. Touch and keyboard still work the same.

The moving petal reverses direction at the edges automatically. You only control the timing of the drop.
