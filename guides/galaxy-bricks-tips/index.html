Galaxy Bricks - Tips, Tricks & How to Get a High Score

Guide · Arcade

In Galaxy Bricks, a classic arcade game, players control a paddle to bounce a ball and destroy bricks in space. The goal is to clear all bricks on the screen while avoiding obstacles and challenges.

  1. Pay attention to the ball's trajectory and speed to anticipate where it will bounce next
  2. Use the paddle's movement to control the ball's direction and aim for specific bricks
  3. Target the bricks at the top of the screen first to create openings and increase your chances of clearing the level
  4. Look out for power-ups and bonuses that can give you an advantage, such as increased paddle size or multiple balls
  5. Time your paddle movements to catch the ball at the right moment, reducing the risk of missing it and losing a life
  6. Identify and prioritize bricks that are most difficult to reach or require a specific angle to hit
  7. Manage your lives and try to conserve them by avoiding unnecessary risks and focusing on clearing the bricks efficiently

By mastering the paddle's movement, anticipating the ball's trajectory, and developing a strategic approach to clearing the bricks, you can overcome the challenges of Galaxy Bricks and achieve a high score.
