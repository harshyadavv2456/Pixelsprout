About Pixelsprout

Last updated: 28-07-2026

Pixelsprout is a free browser gaming library — thousands of games across every genre, playable instantly with no downloads, no installs, and no accounts. Our goal is simple: one tab, every game.

What we offer

Our catalog includes original, self-built games we own outright (2048, Snake, Memory Match, and others), alongside a large library of third-party HTML5 games made available through GameDistribution and GamePix's publisher networks. Every game on the site is reviewed for basic quality before being added.

Who runs this

Pixelsprout is built and operated by an individual developer based in India. It isn't a large company — it's a small, independently run project focused on making casual browser gaming easy and accessible for anyone.

How we're funded

Pixelsprout is supported by advertising (Google AdSense and our third-party game network partners). We don't charge users anything, and we never will — the site is, and will remain, 100% free to use.

Our commitment

We aim to keep the catalog family-friendly and free of adult content, violence, or hate speech. If you ever come across content that shouldn't be here, please let us know and we'll review it promptly.
