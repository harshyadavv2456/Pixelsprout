Cold Read

Tools · Guessing Game

Fix something in your mind - a person, animal, object, or food - and Cold Read asks a series of yes/no/probably questions to narrow it down by elimination. There's no AI model doing the guessing under the hood: it's a plain weighted-scoring algorithm running against a shared dataset that gets a little sharper every time someone plays, whether the guess lands or someone teaches it something new. The dataset starts with a curated list of well-known people, characters, animals, objects, and food - and grows from there.
